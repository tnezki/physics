1. A force is described as 30 N to the right. What information makes this a vector description? | Both magnitude and direction
2. On one vector scale, what does a longer force arrow represent? | A greater force magnitude
3. Two force arrows have the same length but point in opposite directions. Which statement is true? | They have equal magnitudes but different directions.
4. A sled has 25 N right and 60 N right. What is the net force? | 85 N right
5. An 80 N leftward force opposes a 65 N rightward force. What is the resultant? | 15 N left
6. Using right as positive, what is +80 N + (-55 N)? | 25 N right
7. Forces of 35 N right, 10 N right, and 20 N left act on an object. What is the net force? | 25 N right
8. An object has a 35 N resultant to the right. What single force would balance it? | 35 N left
9. A vector diagram represents 20 N left and 50 N right. What is the horizontal resultant? | 30 N right
10. A vector diagram represents 40 N left and 40 N right. What is the horizontal resultant? | 0 N
11. A 60 N rightward force opposes a 25 N leftward force. What is the net force? | 35 N right
12. On a common scale, how should a 60 N force arrow compare with a 20 N force arrow? | It should be three times as long.
13. A 60 N rightward force and a 25 N leftward force act on an object. If the 25 N force reverses direction, what is the new net force? | 85 N right
14. Using right as positive, a resultant is -15 N. How should it be reported? | 15 N left
15. Why is "40 N" alone incomplete as a force-vector description? | A direction is also required.
16. Two horizontal forces are 18 N right and 27 N right. What is the resultant? | 45 N right
17. A 70 N rightward force opposes a 30 N leftward force. What is the resultant? | 40 N right
18. A 40 N resultant points right. Which balancing force would make the horizontal net force zero? | 40 N left
