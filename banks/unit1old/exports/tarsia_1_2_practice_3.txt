1. A suitcase moves in a straight line at constant speed. What does Newton's First Law imply about the net force? | The net force can be zero even though individual forces act.
2. A cart is speeding up to the right. What must be true about its net force? | It is nonzero and has a rightward component.
3. A car moves at constant speed around a curve. Which statement is correct? | Its velocity changes direction, so the net force is not zero.
4. An air-hockey puck remains at rest until it is struck. Which explanation best uses Newton's First Law? | With zero net force, an object at rest remains at rest.
5. A plate tends to stay in place when a tablecloth is pulled quickly from under it. Which idea best explains this? | Inertia—the plate resists a change in its state of motion.
6. Which object has greater inertia? | A loaded cart with greater mass
7. Which statement about inertia is correct? | Inertia is a property of matter, not an extra force.
8. Does a stationary bowling ball have inertia? | Yes. Inertia depends on mass, not whether the object is moving.
9. Two carts receive similar brief pushes. One cart is much more massive. Which is expected to change speed less? | The more massive cart because it has greater inertia.
10. A laptop rests on a desk. Which two main vertical forces act on the laptop? | Weight downward and desk support upward
11. Two students pull a cart with equal forces in opposite directions. What is the best description? | Forces are present, but the horizontal net force is zero.
12. A crate is pulled 45 N right and 45 N left. What is the horizontal net force? | 0 N
13. A box moves across a rough floor at constant speed while someone pushes it. Which horizontal force relationship is consistent with the motion? | The applied push and friction balance.
14. A person stands still on a scale. Which statement is correct about the person? | The upward scale force balances the person's weight.
15. An idealized space probe far from significant interactions moves at constant velocity. What is required to keep it moving? | No continuing net force is required.
16. A book rests on a table. If the table support suddenly disappears, what is the initial net-force direction? | Downward
17. Which example best represents approximately no force rather than several forces that cancel? | An isolated object far from significant interactions
18. Two nonzero horizontal forces are equal in magnitude and opposite in direction. What is their resultant? | 0 N
