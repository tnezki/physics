1. A motion sensor records a cart 0.42 m from the detector. What type of scientific statement is this? | Observation
2. A student says a warmer dark plate probably absorbed more radiant energy. What type of statement is the student making? | Inference
3. A team proposes, 'Increasing ramp height will increase a cart's speed at the bottom.' What type of statement is this? | Hypothesis
4. A compact relationship summarizes a repeatable pattern between measured quantities without explaining the underlying cause. Which category best fits? | Scientific law
5. A broad model explains many related observations and has survived extensive testing. Which category best fits? | Scientific theory
6. Which statement correctly compares scientific laws and theories? | Laws summarize patterns, while theories provide broad evidence-based explanations.
7. Which claim is scientifically testable as stated? | Rougher surfaces make an otherwise identical sliding block stop in a shorter distance.
8. Why is the claim "A crystal has an energy that can never affect any measurement" not scientifically testable? | No possible observation could count for or against it.
9. To test whether pendulum length affects period, which pair should be the independent and dependent variables? | Change length; measure period.
10. A claim says greater release height always gives a greater bottom speed. Which result would be evidence against the claim? | Under controlled conditions, a higher release height repeatedly gives the same or lower bottom speed.
11. When comparing identical dark and light plates in sunlight, which variable is most important to keep the same for a fair comparison? | The plates' material and exposed area
12. A cart reaches 0.6 m/s after a 1 s push and 1.2 m/s after a 2 s push under similar conditions. What conclusion is best supported? | The data do not support the claim that push duration has no effect.
13. Loads of 1, 2, 3, and 4 N stretch a spring about 1.1, 2.1, 3.2, and 4.2 cm. Which description best fits the evidence? | Stretch increases approximately in proportion to load over these trials.
14. A toy car posts times of 2.1 s, 2.0 s, and 2.2 s. Which conclusion is scientifically justified? | The measurements show small trial-to-trial variation, not evidence of intention.
15. For steady speed, a model uses d = vt. If v stays fixed and t doubles, what does the model predict? | Distance doubles.
16. Using right as positive, a net force calculation gives -18 N. What does the negative sign mean? | The resultant is 18 N to the left.
17. A lamp is moved farther from a card and the measured shadow becomes smaller in each tested position. What is the strongest conclusion? | The trials support a decrease in shadow size as lamp distance increases over the tested range.
18. A circle model uses C = 2πr. What happens to C if r doubles? | C doubles.
