1.2 · What's to Come · U1-S2-IC1 · DOK 3

Teacher answer

1.3 · What's to Come · U1-S3-IC3 · DOK 3

Teacher answer

1.4 · What's to Come · U1-S4-IC3 · DOK 3
A platform has 900 N total downward force. One support pushes upward with 350 N and a second support provides the rest. Classify the platform if it is motionless, calculate the second support force, explain how several nonzero forces can coexist at rest, and state what changes if the platform later moves upward at constant velocity.
Teacher answer
Static equilibrium initially; second support is 550 N upward; forces cancel to zero net force. At constant upward velocity it is dynamic equilibrium and the net force remains zero.
1.5 · What's to Come · U1-S5-IC1 · DOK 3
A cart moves to the right at constant velocity on a rough level floor while a steady horizontal push is applied. Draw a force diagram for the cart, use the motion evidence to decide whether the forces are balanced, critique the claim that a separate "motion force" must point right, and state what must change for the cart to speed up to the right.
Teacher answer
A correct model includes weight, support, applied push, and friction; constant velocity means zero net force; no separate motion force exists; speeding up right requires a net force to the right.
