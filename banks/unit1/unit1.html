Unit Mastery Goals

  • U1-MG01: Use evidence and models to distinguish observations, inferences, and testable physics explanations, and communicate what a representation or relationship means.
  • U1-MG02: Explain Newton's First Law and inertia to predict when an object's motion remains unchanged or changes.
  • U1-MG03: Represent and determine net force using vectors, then use the result to classify balanced and unbalanced situations.
  • U1-MG04: Create, critique, and defend force models for real-world systems using evidence about interactions and motion.

1.4 Equilibrium and Balanced Motion

Learning Target: Students will determine whether an object is in mechanical equilibrium and explain how balanced forces relate to rest or constant straight-line motion.

What's To Come
