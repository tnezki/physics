1. A person stands still on a level floor. Which two main forces belong on the person's force diagram? | Weight down and floor support up
2. A sign hangs motionless from one vertical cord. Which forces act on the sign? | Weight down and cord tension up
3. A lamp hangs from two slanted cords. Which force diagram description is correct? | Weight down plus two tensions directed along the cords
4. A crate is held at rest on a rough floor by a slanted rope. Which set of forces can act on the crate? | Weight, floor support, rope tension, and static friction
5. A wagon rolls right and slows after the pull has ended. Which horizontal force is most consistent with its force diagram? | A resistive/friction force to the left
6. A force diagram for a book includes the book's downward push on the table as a force on the book. What is wrong? | That force acts on the table, not on the book.
7. A student adds a forward "motion force" because a puck is moving forward. What is the correct critique? | Motion direction is not itself a force; only real interactions belong on the diagram.
8. A puck glides straight at steady velocity. What does the motion imply about the overall force balance? | The forces are balanced; net force is zero.
9. A toy car increases speed to the right. Which conclusion fits? | Forces are unbalanced with a rightward net force.
10. A cart has 40 N right and 40 N left. What is the horizontal force condition? | Balanced; net force is 0 N.
11. A sled has 55 N right and 20 N left. What is the horizontal net force? | 35 N right
12. A hanging sign remains motionless. What must be true about its net force? | The net force is zero.
13. A student says constant speed always means equilibrium. Which example disproves the claim? | An object moving at constant speed around a curve
14. A cart moves at constant velocity, but a model shows 80 N right and 20 N left as the only horizontal forces. What is the problem? | The drawn forces give a 60 N rightward net force, inconsistent with constant velocity.
15. A 60 N rightward force opposes a 25 N leftward force. What is the resultant? | 35 N right
16. A 60 N leftward force arrow and a 20 N rightward arrow are drawn the same length on one scale. What should be corrected? | The 60 N arrow should be three times the length of the 20 N arrow.
17. What does zero net force mean for velocity? | Velocity remains constant; it does not have to be zero.
18. A motionless hanging lamp model shows only weight downward. What force is missing? | Upward tension/support from the cord or cords
