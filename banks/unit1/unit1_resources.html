A student models a book resting on a table with the diagram below. The student claims, “The downward arrow is the force the book exerts on the table, so the two arrows cancel on the book.” (a) Define the system being modeled. (b) Identify what is wrong with the student’s explanation. (c) Revise the force labels so the model contains only forces acting on the book. (d) Use the revised model to explain why the book can remain at rest.
Teacher answer / alignment

Answer: (a) The system is the book. (b) A force the book exerts on the table acts on the table, not on the book. (c) The forces on the book are its weight downward and the table’s support force upward. (d) Those two forces can be equal and opposite, giving zero net force, so the book remains at rest.
