PM IMPROVEMENT REPORT
========================================================================
PM executed: 3j_Upgrade_Physics_Bank-Approved_Question_Types_PM v1.0

1. Problem encountered.
The completed Physics Unit 1 Bank still contained repeated generic quantitative shells such as force values acting on an unnamed “system,” plus a smaller set of formula-first/equation-first prompts for an evidence/model I-can. Those items could be answered as abstract arithmetic instead of reasoning from a concrete physical situation or measurement/model comparison.

2. Evidence it is systemic/repeatable rather than artifact-specific.
The same failure class appeared in 67 canonical items across four I-cans (U1-S1-IC3, U1-S3-IC2, U1-S4-IC3, U1-S5-IC2) and across Notes, Practice Sets 1-3, Extra Practice, CYU, Warm-Ups, Exit Tickets, and all six secure summative-version families represented by the repaired summative items. Repeated force-value-first stems occurred across multiple instructional destinations, showing that the pattern comes from generation/authoring behavior rather than one isolated artifact.

3. Owning layer.
Physics Bank authoring-quality / Base Bank generation behavior, with a matching finalizer lint rule. The Physics Framework evidence plans are already adequate and do not need content changes for this defect.

4. Exact proposed rule/change.
Add a Physics-specific quantitative-task guardrail:
- For application, model, calculation, balance/equilibrium, and evidence-evaluation families, require the quantities to arise from a concrete physical object/phenomenon, measurement setup, diagram, or experiment rather than a naked variable/force list or unnamed “system.”
- For evidence/model I-cans, require the prompt to use actual data/model output to justify, reject, compare, or limit a conclusion; bare formula manipulation alone does not satisfy the evidence job.
- Allow concise direct Reading Check, vector-language, symbol-meaning, and representation-reading tasks when the exact I-can/evidence family calls for them; do not impose a blanket context quota.
- Add a finalizer review lint for repeated starts such as “A system has…,” “Forces are…,” standalone signed-force lists, or equation-first shells when the resolved i_can_id/evidence family is application/model reasoning. Use an I-can/family allowlist to avoid false positives.

5. Expected benefit / bottleneck removed.
Future Banks should arrive with Physics quantities embedded in meaningful phenomena and with evidence/model questions actually doing evidence/model reasoning, reducing downstream surgical rewrites while preserving concise direct concept checks where they are instructionally appropriate.

6. Possible collateral impact.
An over-broad lint could falsely flag legitimate Plug and Chug, Reading Check, vector-language, or diagram-interpretation questions. The rule must be evidence-family-aware and should review/flag questionable shells rather than mechanically adding decorative nouns or generic explanation tails.

7. Recommendation.
ACCEPT as a narrow Physics Bank authoring/finalizer upgrade. Do not change the Framework learning architecture or force every item into a story context; enforce meaningful physical grounding only where the resolved evidence job calls for applied/model reasoning.
