Physics · First 4 Days

Day 1 Warm-Up

Familiar thinking + opening scientific reasoning
4 prompts
1. Brain teaser

A physics teacher has three identical opaque boxes. One contains a bell that rings when the box is tilted, one contains a sliding metal block, and one contains nothing. Without opening the boxes, what observations could help you identify all three?

2. Formula thinking

A foam alien is dropped from the school bleachers and falls 18 m in 2.0 s. Use $d=\tfrac12at^2$ to estimate its acceleration. Rearrange the relationship for $a$ before substituting the measurements.

3. General science

Two groups measure the same lab table as 1.48 m and 1.50 m. What is one reasonable scientific response to the small difference before deciding either group is wrong?

4. Course routine

Your teacher has just demonstrated how today’s file should be opened, named, and saved. Record the three steps you need to repeat next time without help.
