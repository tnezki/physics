Physics · First 4 Days

Activity Teacher Guide

Normal Activity family: two 24-question pools, projectable participation structures, Stations derivatives, and two Find Someone Who handouts.
Learning TargetI can successfully participate in physics class by following course procedures and using evidence-based reasoning to explain physical phenomena.
Question voicePhenomenon/context first; equations describe the situation rather than acting as naked plug-in prompts.
Participation goalStudents explain evidence and physical relationships to more than one peer.

Choosing a structure

StructureBest use
Whiteboard Indy / ShowdownFast individual evidence of what each student can do.
Whiteboard Partners / Rally Coach / Think–Trade–AgreeSlow down explanation and comparison of reasoning.
Speed Dating / Find Someone WhoGet acquainted while requiring repeated short physics explanations.
Round Table / Hot SeatBuild or defend a complete group explanation.
StationsSustained mixed practice with movement.

Teacher moves

  • When a student jumps directly to a formula, ask: “What is happening physically? What do these numbers describe?”
  • When students call a conclusion evidence, ask: “What did you actually observe or measure?”
  • When reasoning repeats the evidence, ask: “What physical relationship makes that observation support your claim?”
  • Use Set 2 as a genuine parallel pool, not merely a makeup copy.

Student moves

  • State the phenomenon/question before choosing evidence.
  • Point to a measurement, observation, or physical relationship when challenged.
  • Revise after comparing explanations instead of merely copying a peer answer.
