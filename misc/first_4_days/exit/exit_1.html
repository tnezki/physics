Course Routines + CER · Day 1
Portfolio Baseline
1

During a ball-drop trial, one partner records each measurement as soon as it is made. Another says, “I’ll remember the numbers and write them down later.” Which procedure is better for a trustworthy scientific record? Explain why.

2

A student watches a toy car roll down a ramp and writes, “The car moved faster on the steeper ramp.” Is this sentence a Claim, Evidence, or Reasoning? Explain the job of that CER part.

3

A wind-up car travels 1.2 m on tile and 0.7 m on carpet during the same 3 s. Write one clear claim answering: On which surface did the car move faster?

4

Two groups compare how far a toy car rolls, but one group releases the car from a different spot on every trial. Explain why that procedure makes the comparison weaker and describe one change that would make the trials more consistent.
