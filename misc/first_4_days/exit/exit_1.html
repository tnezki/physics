Course Routines + CER · Day 1
Portfolio Baseline
1

Your group measures a toy car. One student records the distance immediately; another says, “I’ll remember it later.” Which procedure better protects the scientific record? Explain why.

2

A battery-powered bug crosses a desk. A student writes, “The bug moved faster on the smooth desk than on the towel.” Is this sentence a Claim, Evidence, or Reasoning? Explain the job of that CER part.

3

A wind-up car travels 38 cm across tile in 2 s and 21 cm across carpet in 2 s. Write one clear claim: On which surface did the car move faster?

4

A student claims a rolling ball moved faster on Ramp A than Ramp B. Which is better evidence: Ramp A was blue, or the ball traveled 1.4 m in 2 s on A and 0.9 m in 2 s on B? Explain why.
