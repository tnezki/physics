Portfolio Baseline 2

Claim + Evidence · Day 2
Portfolio Baseline
1

A toy car is watched for three equal 1-second intervals. It travels 12 cm, then 20 cm, then 31 cm. Write one evidence sentence that supports the claim: The car sped up.

2

A cart travels 24 cm during each of four equal 1-second intervals. A student claims the cart moved at a steady speed. Write one reasoning sentence connecting the measurements to the claim.

3

Claim: A marble rolled faster on the smooth board than on felt. The group recorded board color, a favorite surface, and distances of 82 cm on smooth board and 47 cm on felt during the same 2 s. Identify the relevant evidence and explain why it belongs.

4

A student writes, “The ball sped up because the distances got bigger.” Strengthen the reasoning: what does a larger distance during the same amount of time tell you about speed?
