Claim + Evidence · Day 2
Portfolio Baseline
1

A toy car is observed during three equal 1-second intervals. It travels 12 cm, then 20 cm, then 31 cm. The claim is: The car sped up. Write one evidence sentence using the measurements that supports the claim.

2

Claim: A marble moved farther on the smooth board than on felt during the same time.
The board is blue and the marble has a mass of 18 g. During the same 2 s, the marble travels 82 cm on the smooth board and 47 cm on felt.
Select the evidence that belongs with the claim and explain why it is relevant.

3

A cart travels 24 cm during each of four equal 1-second intervals. A student claims the cart moved at a steady speed. Write one evidence sentence that directly supports the claim.

4

A student writes this evidence for the claim that a ball sped up: “It looked really fast near the end.” Improve the evidence by using these measurements: the ball traveled 8 cm, 15 cm, and 27 cm during three equal 1-second intervals.
