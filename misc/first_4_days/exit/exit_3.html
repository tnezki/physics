Portfolio Baseline
1

A dropped ball is tracked in a video during three equal time intervals. It moves 7 cm, then 15 cm, then 26 cm. Write a complete CER answering: Did the ball speed up as it fell?

2

Claim: The toy car moved faster on tile.
Evidence: It traveled 1.2 m on tile and 0.7 m on carpet during the same 2 s.
Write the Reasoning that explains how the evidence supports the claim.

3

A student writes: “The car sped up because the distances got bigger.” Strengthen the reasoning by explaining what larger distances during equal time intervals tell you about the car’s speed.

4

During video analysis, a group clicks a different point on the ball in every frame. Describe one procedure change that would make the position data more trustworthy and explain why.
