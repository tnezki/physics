Physics · First 4 Days · Teacher

Exit Ticket Keys

Exit Ticket 1 — CER Parts

1. anchor

A battery-powered bug crosses a desk. A student writes, “The bug sped up.” Which CER part is this sentence, and what job does that part do?

Answer / evidence target: Claim; it directly answers the question about the phenomenon.

2. parallel-a

During equal half-second intervals, a cart travels 0.18 m, 0.27 m, and 0.39 m. Write one clear claim about how its speed changes.

Answer / evidence target: The cart sped up.

3. parallel-b

Claim: The dropped ball sped up. Choose stronger evidence: “it looked faster” or “it moved 0.06 m early and 0.19 m later during equal frame intervals.” Explain your choice.

Answer / evidence target: The measured equal-time displacement comparison is stronger because it is specific and relevant.

4. variation

A model car travels farther during each equal time interval. Write one reasoning sentence that explains why this pattern supports a claim that the car sped up.

Answer / evidence target: Speed is distance per unit time; greater distance in the same time means greater speed.

Exit Ticket 2 — Evidence + Reasoning

1. anchor

A foam ball is tracked in slow motion. Its frame-to-frame displacement grows from 0.05 m to 0.17 m while the time between frames stays the same. Write the strongest evidence sentence for the claim “the ball sped up.”

Answer / evidence target: The ball covered 0.17 m in the same frame time later compared with 0.05 m earlier.

2. parallel-a

A student claims a cart moved at constant speed. It travels 0.40 m during each of four equal 0.50 s intervals. Explain why those measurements support the claim.

Answer / evidence target: Equal distance in equal time gives the same speed.

3. parallel-b

A scooter changes from 3 m/s east to 3 m/s west. Explain why this is evidence that velocity changed even though speed stayed 3 m/s.

Answer / evidence target: Velocity includes direction; the direction reversal changes velocity.

4. variation

A group records the cart color, operator name, and changing speed measurements. Which information should appear as evidence for a claim that the cart accelerated, and why?

Answer / evidence target: Changing speed/velocity measurements; they directly bear on acceleration.

Exit Ticket 3 — Mini CER

1. anchor

A foam ball is dropped near a meter stick. During equal video-frame intervals it falls 0.04 m, then 0.10 m, then 0.18 m. Write a complete CER answering: “Did the ball speed up?”

Answer / evidence target: Claim: yes. Evidence: displacement increased across equal frame intervals. Reasoning: greater distance in equal time means greater speed.

2. parallel-a

A model car crosses a 6 m strip in 2 s during one trial and 4 s during another. Write a claim and evidence sentence answering which trial was faster.

Answer / evidence target: Claim: 2 s trial faster. Evidence: same 6 m distance took half the time.

3. parallel-b

A student writes, “The cart accelerated because the speed numbers got bigger.” Revise the reasoning so it explains the physical relationship rather than simply repeating the evidence.

Answer / evidence target: Acceleration is a change in velocity over time; increasing speed measurements show the velocity magnitude changed over time.

4. variation

Name one class procedure that helps make a video-analysis result or data record more trustworthy, then explain how it helps.

Answer / evidence target: Examples: consistent scale/origin/tracking point/file naming; it makes measurements comparable/traceable/repeatable.
