Unit 1 Forces, Motion, and Equilibrium
  1. Net force: $\Sigma F = F_{net}$
  2. Equilibrium rule: $\Sigma F = 0$
  3. Weight near Earth: $W = mg$
  4. Simple support-force balance on a level surface: $F_N = W$ when the object is at rest and no other vertical forces act.
  5. Same-direction forces: $F_{net}=F_1+F_2$
  6. Opposite-direction forces: $F_{net}=F_{larger}-F_{smaller}$, directed toward the larger force.
Unit 2 Motion and Kinematics
  1. Average speed: $v=\frac{d}{t}$
  2. Distance at constant speed: $d=vt$
  3. Time from distance and speed: $t=\frac{d}{v}$
  4. Acceleration: $a=\frac{\Delta v}{t}$
  5. Change in velocity: $\Delta v=v_f-v_i$
  6. Free-fall speed from rest: $v=gt$
  7. Free-fall distance from rest: $d=\frac{1}{2}gt^2$
  8. Average velocity for constant acceleration: $v_{avg}=\frac{v_i+v_f}{2}$
Unit 3 Forces and Motion
  1. Newton's Second Law: $F_{net}=ma$
  2. Acceleration from net force and mass: $a=\frac{F_{net}}{m}$
  3. Mass from force and acceleration: $m=\frac{F_{net}}{a}$
  4. Weight near Earth: $W=mg$
  5. Net force with friction in one dimension: $F_{net}=F_{applied}-F_f$
  6. Falling with air resistance: $F_{net}=W-F_{air}$
  7. Horizontal projectile motion: $x=v_xt$
  8. Vertical drop from rest: $y=\frac{1}{2}gt^2$
Unit 4 Momentum and Energy
  1. Momentum: $p=mv$
  2. Impulse: $J=Ft$
  3. Impulse-momentum relationship: $J=\Delta p$
  4. Conservation of momentum: $p_{before}=p_{after}$
  5. Work: $W=Fd$
  6. Power: $P=\frac{W}{t}$
  7. Gravitational potential energy: $PE=mgh$
  8. Kinetic energy: $KE=\frac{1}{2}mv^2$
Unit 5 Waves and Sound
  1. Wave speed: $v=f\lambda$
  2. Frequency from period: $f=\frac{1}{T}$
  3. Period from frequency: $T=\frac{1}{f}$
  4. Speed from distance and time: $v=\frac{d}{t}$
  5. Wavelength from wave speed and frequency: $\lambda=\frac{v}{f}$
  6. Beat frequency: $f_{beat}=|f_1-f_2|$
  7. Echo distance relationship: $d=\frac{vt}{2}$
  8. Approximate speed of sound in air: $v_{sound}\approx 340\text{ m/s}$
Unit 6 Light and Optics
  1. Electromagnetic wave speed: $c=f\lambda$
  2. Index of refraction: $n=\frac{c}{v}$
  3. Law of reflection: $\theta_i=\theta_r$
  4. Snell's Law: $n_1\sin\theta_1=n_2\sin\theta_2$
  5. Critical angle relationship: $\sin\theta_c=\frac{n_2}{n_1}$, when light travels from a higher-index medium to a lower-index medium
  6. Thin lens equation: $\frac{1}{f}=\frac{1}{d_o}+\frac{1}{d_i}$
  7. Magnification: $M=\frac{h_i}{h_o}$
  8. Light travel speed relationship: $v=\frac{d}{t}$
Unit 7 Electricity and Circuits
  1. Coulomb's Law: $F=k\frac{q_1q_2}{r^2}$
  2. Electric potential difference: $V=\frac{E}{q}$
  3. Electric current: $I=\frac{Q}{t}$
  4. Ohm's Law: $V=IR$
  5. Electric power: $P=IV$
  6. Electrical energy use: $E=Pt$
  7. Series equivalent resistance: $R_{eq}=R_1+R_2+\cdots$
  8. Parallel equivalent resistance: $\frac{1}{R_{eq}}=\frac{1}{R_1}+\frac{1}{R_2}+\cdots$
Unit 8 Magnetism and Electromagnetism
  1. Magnetic force on a moving charge: $F=qvB$
  2. Magnetic force on a current-carrying wire: $F=BIL$
  3. Magnetic field strength from force on a moving charge: $B=\frac{F}{qv}$
  4. Magnetic field strength from force on a wire: $B=\frac{F}{IL}$
  5. Faraday's Law concept relationship: induced voltage increases when the rate of magnetic-field change increases
  6. Transformer relationship: $\frac{V_s}{V_p}=\frac{N_s}{N_p}$
  7. Power relationship for ideal transformers: $P_{in}\approx P_{out}$
  8. Electric power: $P=IV$
