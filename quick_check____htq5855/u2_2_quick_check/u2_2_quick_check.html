2.2 · A
Name: __________________________________
Date: ______________
Period: ______

Answer all four questions independently. Show calculations for speed problems. Include units.

1
A cart travels 18 m in 6 s. Calculate its average speed and include units.
2
A toy car rolls from 0 to 4 ft down your ramp. A second iPad films the car and a running stopwatch. The filmed stopwatch shows these readings:
Distance from startStopwatch reading
0 ft2.00 s
1 ft4.00 s
2 ft5.00 s
3 ft5.60 s
4 ft6.00 s

a. Find the time from 1 to 2 ft, then calculate average speed for that one-foot section (ft/s).

b. Which one-foot section, 0–1, 1–2, 2–3, or 3–4 ft, was fastest?

3
During a 12-minute bicycle trip, the rider’s average speed is 7 m/s. At one instant, the speedometer reads 10 m/s. Distinguish the two measurements.
4
A robot moves at a steady 9 m/s south. Explain what makes this a velocity description rather than only a speed description, and state what would make the velocity change even if the speed stayed 9 m/s.
