Current State

Read-only student preview of the manually selected current week. Quick Check stays display-only for students; test-day labels such as Unit 1 Test appear as plain agenda text.

Planner Editor – All Weeks

Every week is editable. Use Bump Forward to insert a blank instructional day at that date and move the remaining instructional sequence forward one school day. Insert Blank Day remains as the same forward-shift action; Bump Back removes one instructional day and pulls the sequence back. Planned blank days between units are automatically labeled Unit 1 Test, Unit 2 Test, and so on, with an Approved Assessments teacher link. Use Custom Day Label... only for other special day labels. For anything not in a resource dropdown, choose Custom Item....

Save + Update Student Agenda saves Planner changes and refreshes the Physics student and co-teacher agendas. Use the separate GitHub Sync app when you are ready to pull, commit, or push.
