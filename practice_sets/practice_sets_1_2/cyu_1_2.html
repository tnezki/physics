Conceptual Physics Check Your Understanding 1.2

1. A hockey puck glides across nearly frictionless ice at constant velocity after the stick is no longer touching it. Which principle explains why no continuing forward net force is required?

  1. Newton’s First Law
  2. inertia force
  3. Newton’s Third Law
  4. gravitational law

2. State Newton’s First Law in everyday language for a student who has not yet learned the formal wording.

3. A passenger’s body tends to continue moving forward when a car stops suddenly. What property of matter does this illustrate?

  1. inertia
  2. net force
  3. support force
  4. friction

4. Explain what inertia is and why it should not be drawn as an extra force on a force diagram.

5. Use the book-on-table force diagram to identify the individual forces. Which statement best describes the forces on the book?

  1. Weight acts down and support force acts up; they can balance.
  2. Only weight acts because the book is not moving.
  3. Only support force acts because the table holds the book.
  4. Inertia acts upward to cancel gravity.

6. A book rests on a table. Name the two most important vertical forces acting on the book and state whether “no forces act” or “no net force acts” is the correct description.
