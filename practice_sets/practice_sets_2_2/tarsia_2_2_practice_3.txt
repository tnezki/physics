TARSIA PAIRS — Section 2.2
01	A cart travels 20 m in 4 s. Calculate its average speed and include units.	5 m/s
02	A cart travels 64 m in 8 s. Calculate its average speed and include units.	8 m/s
03	A robot moves at a steady 6 m/s north. Explain what makes this a velocity description rather than only a speed description, and state what would make the velocity change even if the speed stayed 6 m/s.	It gives both speed (6 m/s) and direction (north). The velocity changes if the direction changes, even if the speed remains 6 m/s.
04	During a 12-minute bicycle trip, the rider’s average speed is 7 m/s. At one instant, the speedometer reads 10 m/s. Distinguish the two measurements.	7 m/s describes the whole trip average; 10 m/s is the instantaneous speed at that moment.
