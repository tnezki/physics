Conceptual Physics Check Your Understanding 1.5

1. A picture frame hangs motionless from two angled cords. Draw a force diagram for the frame only.

2. A skateboarder coasts to the right on level ground and slows because of resistive forces. Draw a force diagram for the skateboarder-plus-board system.

3. The cart is described as moving at constant speed, but inspect the supplied force arrows. Are the forces balanced or unbalanced? Cite the mismatch.

  1. Unbalanced: the unequal opposing horizontal arrows imply a nonzero net force, which conflicts with constant-speed straight-line motion.
  2. Balanced because any object with two or more forces is automatically in equilibrium.
  3. Unbalanced because motion always requires a forward net force.
  4. No conclusion is possible from a force diagram.

4. Inspect the crate’s force diagram. Determine whether the opposing horizontal forces are balanced or unbalanced, and cite the arrow evidence.

5. A student says, “The cart is moving right, so the net force must point right.” Critique and revise the explanation using physics vocabulary.

6. A rolling cart moves right while the net force points left. Predict how its motion changes while that net force acts. Justify using net force.
