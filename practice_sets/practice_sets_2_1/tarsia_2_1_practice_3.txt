TARSIA PAIRS — Section 2.1
01	A rider on a train says a bag on the floor is at rest. An observer standing on the ground says the bag is moving with the train. Compare the two descriptions and explain why Earth’s surface is a convenient frame for everyday travel.	Both descriptions can be correct because they use different reference frames. Earth’s surface is a stable, shared everyday reference frame for locations and travel.
02	A description says, “The backpack stays beside Mina on the seat while roadside signs move backward past the window.” Identify the reference frame used when the backpack is described as staying beside Mina, and cite the cue.	The vehicle/passenger frame; the object keeps a fixed position relative to the passenger and seat.
03	Ari walks toward the front of a train moving east at 10 m/s. Ari walks east at 3 m/s relative to the train. Explain how Ari can have one velocity relative to the train and a different velocity relative to the ground.	Relative to the train, Ari moves east at 3 m/s; relative to the ground, Ari moves east at 13 m/s. Motion depends on the chosen reference frame.
