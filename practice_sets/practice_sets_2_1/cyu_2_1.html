Section 2.1 — Check Your Understanding

1. Kai walks toward the front of a train moving east at 9 m/s. Kai walks at 2 m/s relative to the train. Explain how Kai can be moving relative to the ground while also being described using a different motion relative to the train.

  1. Kai must have the same velocity in every reference frame.
  2. Kai is at rest relative to the ground because the train is the only valid frame.
  3. Reference frames change the object’s mass, which changes its motion.
  4. Relative to the train, Kai moves east at 2 m/s; relative to the ground, Kai moves east at 11 m/s. Motion depends on the chosen reference frame.

2. Sam walks toward the front of a train moving east at 10 m/s. Sam walks at 3 m/s relative to the train. Explain how Sam can be moving relative to the ground while also being described using a different motion relative to the train.

3. A motion description says, “The backpack remains beside Zoe on the seat while trees move backward past the window.” Identify the reference frame used when the backpack is described as remaining beside Zoe, and cite the cue.

  1. no reference frame is needed
  2. the vehicle/passenger frame
  3. the trees outside
  4. the backpack itself only

4. A motion description says, “The backpack remains beside Ari on the seat while trees move backward past the window.” Identify the reference frame used when the backpack is described as remaining beside Ari, and cite the cue.

5. A passenger on a bus says a phone on the seat is at rest. A person on the sidewalk says the phone is moving east with the bus. Compare the two descriptions and explain why using Earth’s surface is convenient for ordinary travel descriptions.

  1. Only the passenger can be correct because the phone is touching the seat.
  2. Only the sidewalk observer can be correct because Earth is the unique true frame.
  3. Both must measure zero velocity because the phone is not sliding.
  4. Both descriptions can be correct because they use different reference frames. The phone is at rest in the bus frame and moving in the Earth/sidewalk frame. Earth’s surface is a convenient shared everyday frame.
