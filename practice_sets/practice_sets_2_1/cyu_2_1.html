Section 2.1 — Check Your Understanding

1. Nia walks toward the front of a train moving east at 10 m/s. Nia walks east at 2 m/s relative to the train. Explain how Nia can have one velocity relative to the train and a different velocity relative to the ground.

2. Kai walks toward the front of a train moving east at 8 m/s. Kai walks east at 2 m/s relative to the train. Explain how Kai can have one velocity relative to the train and a different velocity relative to the ground.

  1. Kai must have the same velocity in every frame.
  2. The train is the only valid frame.
  3. Changing reference frame changes the object’s mass.
  4. Relative to the train, Kai moves east at 2 m/s; relative to the ground, Kai moves east at 10 m/s. Motion depends on the chosen reference frame.

3. A description says, “The book stays beside Zoe on the seat while roadside signs move backward past the window.” Identify the reference frame used when the book is described as staying beside Zoe, and cite the cue.

4. A description says, “The cup stays beside Nia on the seat while roadside signs move backward past the window.” Identify the reference frame used when the cup is described as staying beside Nia, and cite the cue.

  1. the roadside signs
  2. the object by itself
  3. no frame is needed
  4. the vehicle/passenger frame

5. A rider on a ferry says a bag on the floor is at rest. An observer standing on the ground says the bag is moving with the ferry. Compare the two descriptions and explain why Earth’s surface is a convenient frame for everyday travel.

6. A rider on a train says a bag on the floor is at rest. An observer standing on the ground says the bag is moving with the train. Compare the two descriptions and explain why Earth’s surface is a convenient frame for everyday travel.

  1. Only the rider can be correct.
  2. Only the ground observer can be correct.
  3. Both must measure zero speed.
  4. Both descriptions can be correct because they use different reference frames. Earth’s surface is a stable, shared everyday reference frame for locations and travel.
