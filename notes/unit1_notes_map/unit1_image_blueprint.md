# Unit 1 Image Blueprint

Bank representations are the primary instructional visuals for this unit. They must be copied from the canonical Bank exactly at Stage 4B. No web image or replacement drawing is permitted. The course-owned toolkit is `resources/Physics_Notes_Image_Toolkit/` v3.1.0; this map does not force a supplemental toolkit image where a Bank representation already carries the instructional load.

## Toolkit Rule for Stage 4B
If a surrounding explanatory paragraph genuinely benefits from a supplemental image, Stage 4B must select an **exact filename** from `resources/Physics_Notes_Image_Toolkit/IMAGE_CATALOG.json`, verify its intended section/tags and provenance, and obey the static-raster minimum of 900×550. A toolkit image may support but never replace a required Bank representation.
