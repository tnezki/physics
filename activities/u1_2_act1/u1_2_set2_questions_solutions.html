Problem 24

A sign weighs 95 N and hangs at rest from a vertical cable. Determine the cable force needed for equilibrium. The cable is then limited to 70 N upward. Does the sign remain in equilibrium?

Solution: At rest, cable force must be 95 N upward. With only 70 N upward, the net force is 25 N downward, so equilibrium is lost.
