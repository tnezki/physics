Question 1

1. Which sentence is the best everyday statement of Newton’s First Law?

  1. Motion stays unchanged unless a nonzero net force acts.
  2. Moving objects always require a forward force.
  3. Objects at rest have no forces.
  4. Inertia is the force that keeps motion going.
Answer: A — Motion stays unchanged unless a nonzero net force acts.
Work / rationale: Newton’s First Law says an object keeps its state of motion unless a nonzero net force changes it.

Question 2

2. Inertia is

  1. a push or pull
  2. a property of matter that resists changes in motion
  3. the vector sum of forces
  4. the force of motion
Answer: B — a property of matter that resists changes in motion
Work / rationale: Inertia is the property of matter that resists a change in velocity; it is not a push or pull.

Question 3

3. A book rests on a table. Which two main vertical forces act on the book?

  1. tension down and friction up
  2. inertia down and net force up
  3. weight down and support force up
  4. support force down and weight up
Answer: C — weight down and support force up
Work / rationale: The book has weight downward from Earth and an upward support force from the table.

  4. a 6-kg cart at rest

Work / rationale: Inertia increases with mass, so the 6 kg cart has more inertia regardless of whether it is moving.

Question 17

Work / rationale: For the lamp to remain motionless, upward tension and downward weight can balance to give zero net force.

Question 18

  3. a hanging sign
  4. a person standing on a floor
Answer: B — a hypothetical object with no interactions at all
Work / rationale: Literally no forces means no interactions at all; ordinary objects on tables, floors, or cables still have interaction forces.
