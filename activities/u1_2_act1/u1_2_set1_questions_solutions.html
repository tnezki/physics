Problem 1

A loaded grocery cart is harder to start or stop than an empty cart. Explain this using inertia.

Solution: The loaded cart has more mass and therefore more inertia, so it resists changes in velocity more strongly.

Problem 2

A parked car rests on level ground. Explain why “no forces act” is incorrect.

Solution: Weight acts downward and the ground’s support force acts upward; the forces balance, so the net force is zero even though forces act.
