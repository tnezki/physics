Problem 24

A sign weighs 180 N and hangs at rest from a vertical cable. Determine the cable force needed for equilibrium. The cable is then limited to 150 N upward. Does the sign remain in equilibrium?

Solution: At rest, cable force must be 180 N upward. With only 150 N upward, the net force is 30 N downward, so equilibrium is lost.
