Question 6

6. An echo returns after 1.2 s. At 340 m/s, how far away is the reflector?

A. 408 m
B. 204 m
C. 102 m
D. 340 m
Answer: B — 204 m
Work / rationale: Round-trip distance is $(340)(1.2)=408\text{ m}$; the reflector is half that distance, $204\text{ m}$ away.

Question 7

7. Why is the echo distance not simply $vt$?

A. Sound travels at half speed on return.
B. The echo frequency is always half.
C. The recorded time includes the trip out and the trip back.
D. The wave has half a wavelength after reflection.
Answer: C — The recorded time includes the trip out and the trip back.
Work / rationale: The measured echo time includes both the outbound and return trips, so one-way distance is $vt/2$.
