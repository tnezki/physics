Problem 1

The dots mark equal time intervals. Describe the motion shown, connect the changing spacing to velocity, and critique the claim “equal time intervals always mean constant velocity.”

Answer: In a motion diagram made at equal time intervals, larger spacing means greater speed. Changing spacing is evidence of changing velocity.

Problem 2

Compare the two velocity vectors. Determine whether the object’s velocity changes from v₁ to v₂ and identify the evidence in the arrows.

  1. The velocity changes because the vectors differ in magnitude, direction, or both.
  2. Velocity cannot change unless the object stops.
  3. Only arrow color matters.
  4. The vectors always represent the same velocity.

Answer: Two velocity vectors are identical only when both magnitude and direction match. Any difference means velocity changed.
