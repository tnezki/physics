Teacher & Student Moves
Section evidence focus
Unit 2
Velocity Vectors and Motion Modeling
I can
  • I can represent velocity with arrows that show both speed and direction.
  • I can compare velocity vectors to determine whether an object’s velocity is changing.
  • I can connect motion diagrams, data tables, and graphs to written descriptions of motion and critique an incorrect motion model and revise it using evidence.
Teacher moves
  • Ask: “What tells you that?”
  • Require the reference frame, units, direction, or representation named in the prompt.
  • Press students to compare models rather than vote.
Student moves
  • State a claim.
  • Use the given data or representation.
  • Explain why the evidence supports the claim.
