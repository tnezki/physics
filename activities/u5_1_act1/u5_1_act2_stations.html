Station 1 Answers

Question 1

1. Which motion is a vibration?

A. A pendulum bob repeating left-right motion
B. A ball moving once down a ramp
C. A car parked at a light
D. A box carried steadily forward
Answer: A — A pendulum bob repeating left-right motion
Work / rationale: A vibration is repeated motion about an equilibrium position; the pendulum repeats left-right motion.

Question 2

2. What is transferred by a traveling mechanical wave?

A. The entire medium from source to receiver
B. Energy
C. Mass equal to one wavelength
D. Only the equilibrium position
Answer: B — Energy
Work / rationale: A traveling mechanical wave transfers energy while the medium particles oscillate locally.

Question 3

3. Which distance is one wavelength on a transverse wave?

A. Equilibrium to crest
B. Crest to neighboring trough
C. Crest to the next crest
D. Source to observer
Answer: C — Crest to the next crest
Work / rationale: One wavelength is the distance between matching points on successive cycles, such as crest to next crest.
