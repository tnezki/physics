A. 15 cm
B. 20 cm
C. 30 cm
D. 40 cm
Answer: A — 15 cm
Work / rationale: $1/d_i=1/10-1/30=2/30=1/15$, so $d_i=15\text{ cm}$.

Question 7

A. 6
B. 0.33
C. 12
D. 3
Answer: D — 3
Work / rationale: $|m|=|h_i/h_o|=9/3=3$.
