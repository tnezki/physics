Question 1

1. A magnetic pole is best described as

  1. an isolated electric charge
  2. a region of a magnet where magnetic interactions are strongest
  3. a place where mass disappears
  4. a physical field line
Answer: B — a region of a magnet where magnetic interactions are strongest
Work / rationale: Magnetic interactions are strongest near pole regions; a pole is not an isolated electric charge or a field line.

Question 2

2. Two facing north poles are brought close together. What happens?

  1. They become electrically neutral.
  2. They form one south pole.
  3. They attract.
  4. They repel.
Answer: D — They repel.
Work / rationale: Like magnetic poles repel, so two north poles push apart.

Question 3

3. Which feature of a magnetic-field diagram represents relative field strength?

  1. how closely the field lines are spaced
  2. the page color
  3. the length of the magnet label
  4. the compass brand
Answer: A — how closely the field lines are spaced
Work / rationale: In a consistently drawn field model, closer field-line spacing represents greater relative field strength.
