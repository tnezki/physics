Problem 1

Two compact objects of masses 2 kg and 6 kg are dropped together in a vacuum from the same height. Predict their accelerations and explain why one does not “fall faster” simply because it is heavier.

Solution: In ideal free fall, gravity produces the same acceleration for all objects regardless of mass.

Problem 5

An object starts from rest and falls freely for 3 s. Using g ≈ 10 m/s², determine the speed gained.

  1. about 30 m/s downward
  2. about 3 m/s downward
  3. about 13 m/s downward
  4. about 30 m/s upward
