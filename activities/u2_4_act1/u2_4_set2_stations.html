Problem 1

An object starts from rest and falls freely for 2 s. Using g ≈ 10 m/s², determine the speed gained.

Solution: For an object starting from rest in ideal free fall, v = gt ≈ (10 m/s²)(2 s) = 20 m/s downward.

Problem 2

Use the ideal free-fall table to compare how distance fallen changes from one second to the next. Then explain why a sheet of paper falling through air may not follow this pattern.

Time (s)Distance fallen (m)
15
220
345

Solution: Ideal free fall follows d = ½gt². Air resistance can reduce acceleration below the ideal value and alter the pattern.
