Problem 5

An object starts from rest and falls freely for 3 s. Using g ≈ 10 m/s², determine the speed gained.

  1. about 30 m/s downward
  2. about 3 m/s downward
  3. about 13 m/s downward
  4. about 30 m/s upward

Solution: For an object starting from rest in ideal free fall, v = gt ≈ (10 m/s²)(3 s) = 30 m/s downward.
