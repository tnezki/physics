Unit 2.4 · Activity 1 · Free Fall MotionSolution 12
Solution 12
DOK 3

12. Compare a coin and feather falling in air with the same pair falling in vacuum. Use the contrast to separate the effects of gravity and air resistance.

Answer / work: In air, the feather is slowed much more by air resistance, so the coin arrives first. In vacuum, air resistance is absent and both objects accelerate at the same gravitational rate, so they fall together.
