Q1

Ignoring air resistance, an object is dropped from rest. Using g ≈ 10 m/s², determine the speed it has gained after 4 s.

  1. 40 m/s upward
  2. 40 m/s downward
  3. 14 m/s downward
  4. 20 m/s downward
S1

Ignoring air resistance, an object is dropped from rest. Using g ≈ 10 m/s², determine the speed it has gained after 4 s.

Answer: 40 m/s downward
Reasoning: Speed gained = g t = (10 m/s²)(4 s) = 40 m/s. Direction is downward.
Q2

A metal sphere and a wooden sphere are dropped together in an evacuated tube. Explain why mass alone does not make one accelerate faster than the other.

S2

A metal sphere and a wooden sphere are dropped together in an evacuated tube. Explain why mass alone does not make one accelerate faster than the other.

Answer: With air resistance removed, both are in ideal free fall and have the same gravitational acceleration near Earth.
Reasoning: In ideal free fall, gravitational acceleration is independent of object mass.
