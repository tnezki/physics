C. $v=dt$
D. $t=dv$

Solution 1
DOK 1

1. Which formula gives average speed?

A. $v=d/t$
B. $d=t/v$
C. $v=dt$
D. $t=dv$
Answer: A — $v=d/t$
Work / rationale: Average speed is defined by total distance divided by total time: $v=d/t$.
