C. 8 m/s west

4. Which quantity is scalar?

A. velocity

C. direction with magnitude
D. speed

5. Constant velocity requires

A. constant speed and constant direction
B. constant speed only
C. constant direction only
D. changing speed and direction

A. 6 m/s
B. 8 m/s
C. 10 m/s
D. 12 m/s

A. 27 m
B. 3.5 m

A. 20 s
B. 24 s

D. 30 s

A. velocity because direction changes
B. speed only
C. neither speed nor velocity

10. Two trips have the same total distance and total time. What must be the same?

A. instantaneous speed at every moment
B. average speed
C. direction throughout
D. number of stops

A. both run north
B. both finish at the same time

D. both use the same shoes

B. Average speed must always exceed instantaneous speed.

B. 6 m/s

A. 12 m/s east
B. 12 m/s

B. Instantaneous uses no units.

17. Which motion has constant speed but not constant velocity?

B. steady straight east motion

A. Speed changes but velocity does not.

A. 10 m/s
B. 25 m/s
