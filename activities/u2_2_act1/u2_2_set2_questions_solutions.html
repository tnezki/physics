Q1

On a warehouse cart, a rider’s speedometer reads 18 m/s at one instant. The rider’s average speed over the entire trip is 9 m/s. Explain what each value describes.

  1. The larger value is average and the smaller value is instantaneous by definition.
  2. 18 m/s is instantaneous speed at that moment; 9 m/s is total distance divided by total trip time.
  3. Both values are instantaneous because both use m/s.
  4. Both values are average because every speed is averaged over time.
S1

On a warehouse cart, a rider’s speedometer reads 18 m/s at one instant. The rider’s average speed over the entire trip is 9 m/s. Explain what each value describes.

Answer: 18 m/s is instantaneous speed at that moment; 9 m/s is total distance divided by total trip time.
Reasoning: Instantaneous speed describes one moment. Average speed combines the whole interval: total distance divided by total elapsed time.

  4. The speed remains 6 m/s, but the velocity changes because its direction changes from east to north.
