Problem 1

A cart travels 20 m in 4 s. Calculate its average speed and include units.

Solution: Average speed = distance ÷ time = 20 m ÷ 4 s = 5 m/s.

Problem 3

A robot moves at a steady 6 m/s north. Explain what makes this a velocity description rather than only a speed description, and state what would make the velocity change even if the speed stayed 6 m/s.

  1. It gives both speed (6 m/s) and direction (north). The velocity changes if the direction changes, even if the speed remains 6 m/s.
