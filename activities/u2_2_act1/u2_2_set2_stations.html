Station 1 Solutions

1. 18 m/s is instantaneous speed at that moment; 9 m/s is total distance divided by total trip time. — Instantaneous speed describes one moment. Average speed combines the whole interval: total distance divided by total elapsed time.
2. The speed remains 6 m/s, but the velocity changes because its direction changes from east to north. — Velocity includes magnitude and direction. A direction change changes velocity even when speed stays constant.
