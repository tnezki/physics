Question 1

1. Which formula gives average speed?

A. $v=d/t$
B. $d=t/v$
C. $v=dt$
D. $t=dv$
Answer: A — $v=d/t$
Work / rationale: Average speed is defined by total distance divided by total time: $v=d/t$.

Question 2

2. Which is an instantaneous speed?

A. total distance divided by total time
B. a speedometer reading right now
C. the average for a whole trip
D. the distance on an odometer
Answer: B — a speedometer reading right now
Work / rationale: A speedometer reading “right now” describes speed at one instant.

Question 3

3. Which description is a velocity?

A. 8 m/s
B. 8 m
C. 8 m/s west
D. west
Answer: C — 8 m/s west
Work / rationale: Velocity requires a magnitude and a direction, both of which appear in “8 m/s west.”
