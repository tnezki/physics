Station 1 Solutions

1. 12 m/s is instantaneous speed at that moment; 10 m/s is total distance divided by total trip time. — Instantaneous speed describes one moment; average speed combines the whole interval.
2. 12 m/s is instantaneous speed at that moment; 10 m/s is total distance divided by total trip time. — Instantaneous speed describes one moment. Average speed combines the whole interval: total distance divided by total elapsed time.
