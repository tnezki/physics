Problem 1

A robot moves at a steady 8 m/s east. Explain what makes this a velocity description rather than only a speed description, and state what would make the velocity change even if the speed stayed 8 m/s.

Answer: Velocity is a vector: magnitude and direction. A turn changes velocity even at constant speed.

Problem 2

During a 12-minute bicycle trip, the rider’s average speed is 6 m/s. At one instant, the speedometer reads 9 m/s. Distinguish the two measurements.

Answer: Average speed uses total distance and total time. Instantaneous speed describes the speed at one specific moment.

Problem 7

A cart travels 49 m in 7 s. Calculate its average speed and include units.

  1. 7 m/s
  2. 56 m/s
  3. 49 m/s
  4. 7 m/s

Answer: Average speed = distance ÷ time = 49 m ÷ 7 s = 7 m/s.
