Question 1

1. What is the primary function of a transformer?

  1. create free power from magnets
  2. change voltage while transferring electrical energy through coupled coils
  3. store charge indefinitely
  4. convert motion directly to electrical energy
Answer: B — change voltage while transferring electrical energy through coupled coils
Work / rationale: A transformer transfers electrical energy between coupled coils while changing voltage according to the turns ratio.

Question 2

2. The input-side transformer coil is called the

  1. secondary coil
  2. generator rotor
  3. motor coil
  4. primary coil
Answer: D — primary coil
Work / rationale: The primary winding is the input-side coil connected to the source.

Question 3

3. The turn ratio has $N_s>N_p$. Which transformer classification follows?

  1. step-up
  2. a generator
  3. step-down
  4. a motor
Answer: A — step-up
Work / rationale: If $N_s>N_p$, then $V_s>V_p$ in the ideal turns-ratio model, so the transformer is step-up.
