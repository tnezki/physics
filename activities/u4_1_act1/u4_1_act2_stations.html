Question 1

1. Momentum depends on mass and

A. velocity
B. contact time
C. force only
D. height
Answer: A — velocity
Work / rationale: Momentum is $p=mv$, so it depends on both mass and velocity.

Question 2

2. An object at rest has momentum

A. equal to its mass
B. zero
C. equal to its weight
D. that cannot be determined
Answer: B — zero
Work / rationale: At rest $v=0$, so $p=mv=0$.

Question 3

3. Impulse is calculated from

A. mass multiplied by velocity
B. work divided by time
C. force multiplied by interaction time
D. force multiplied by distance
Answer: C — force multiplied by interaction time
Work / rationale: Impulse is $J=F\Delta t$ for a constant/average force over the interaction time.

8. Momentum changes from −2 to +7 kg·m/s. The impulse is

A. −9 N·s
B. +5 N·s
C. −5 N·s
D. +9 N·s
Answer: D — +9 N·s
Work / rationale: $\Delta p=p_f-p_i=(+7)-(-2)=+9\text{ kg\cdot m/s}$.

Question 9

9. The same 10 N·s impulse occurs over 0.05 s instead of 0.20 s. The shorter interaction has

A. four times the average force
B. one-fourth the average force
C. the same average force
D. zero average force
Answer: A — four times the average force
Work / rationale: For the same impulse, $F_{avg}=J/\Delta t$. Reducing time from $0.20$ s to $0.05$ s makes the time one-fourth as large, so force is four times as large.

D. 1 kg at rest and 2 kg at 1 m/s
Answer: B — 2 kg at 6 m/s and 4 kg at 3 m/s
Work / rationale: $(2)(6)=12$ and $(4)(3)=12\text{ kg\cdot m/s}$, so those momentum magnitudes match.
