Station 1 Answers

Question 1

1. Newton’s Third Law says forces occur as

A. equal-magnitude opposite-direction interactions on different objects
B. unequal forces on one object
C. equal forces on the same object only
D. forces that always cancel motion
Answer: A — equal-magnitude opposite-direction interactions on different objects
Work / rationale: Newton’s Third Law describes equal-magnitude, opposite-direction forces exerted by two different interacting objects.

Question 2

2. A bat pushes a ball forward. The reaction force is

A. gravity pulling the ball down
B. the ball pushing the bat backward
C. the bat pushing itself backward
D. air pushing the bat forward
Answer: B — the ball pushing the bat backward
Work / rationale: If the bat pushes the ball forward, the ball pushes the bat backward with equal magnitude.

Question 3

3. If object A pushes B with 60 N, B pushes A with

A. 30 N
B. 120 N
C. 60 N in the opposite direction
D. zero
Answer: C — 60 N in the opposite direction
Work / rationale: Third-Law partners have equal magnitude, so B pushes A with $60\text{ N}$ in the opposite direction.
