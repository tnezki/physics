Conceptual Physics • Unit 1 • Section 1.1Activity Set 2 • Solution
Problem 24

A student says, “A theory is just an untested guess that eventually becomes a law.” Critique the statement.

Solution: A scientific theory is a broad, well-supported explanation, not an untested guess; theories do not “turn into” laws because laws describe patterns while theories explain them.
