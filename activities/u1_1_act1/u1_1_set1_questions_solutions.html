Mastery · DOK 1

Perform each rational-number operation, using fraction rules as needed.

  1. $\frac{2}{9}+\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)$
  2. $-\frac{6}{7}-\frac{3}{5}$
  3. $\frac{9}{10}\left(-\frac{2}{3}\right)$
  4. $\frac{1}{4}\div\frac{2}{7}$
Solution:
  1. $\frac{2}{9}-\frac{1}{2}=\frac{4}{18}-\frac{9}{18}=-\frac{5}{18}$.
  2. $-\frac{6}{7}-\frac{3}{5}=-\frac{30}{35}-\frac{21}{35}=-\frac{51}{35}$.
  3. $\frac{9}{10}\left(-\frac{2}{3}\right)=-\frac{18}{30}=-\frac{3}{5}$.
  4. $\frac{1}{4}\div\frac{2}{7}=\frac{1}{4}\cdot\frac{7}{2}=\frac{7}{8}$.

Construct a step-by-step strategy for evaluating $\left|-4.5+\frac{7}{3}\right|-\left(-\frac{5}{6}\right)$. Your strategy must explain when to use absolute value and when to combine rational numbers.

Solution: First combine inside the absolute value: $-4.5+\frac{7}{3}=-\frac{9}{2}+\frac{7}{3}=-\frac{27}{6}+\frac{14}{6}=-\frac{13}{6}$. Then $\left|-\frac{13}{6}\right|=\frac{13}{6}$. Finally subtracting a negative means add: $\frac{13}{6}+\frac{5}{6}=3$.
