Problem 1

Design a test of the claim “A heavier cart takes more time to travel the same ramp distance.” Identify the changed variable, measured variable, and one control.

Solution: Change cart mass, measure travel time over the same ramp distance, and keep a condition such as ramp angle/release point the same.
Problem 2

A student sees that a cart stops sooner on carpet than on tile and concludes that carpet provides more resistance. Classify the conclusion and explain why.

Solution: It is an inference: the student interprets observations (shorter stopping on carpet) to reach a conclusion about resistance.

Problem 3

A student says, “The cart that traveled farther was faster,” even though the two carts were observed for different amounts of time. Critique the reasoning.

Problem 13

A classmate says, “A scientific law is deeper and more certain than a scientific theory.” Critique and revise the claim.

Solution: Laws and theories have different jobs rather than a certainty hierarchy: laws describe consistent patterns; theories explain broad bodies of evidence.
