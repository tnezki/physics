Problem 13

Is the claim “Music makes plants “happier.”” scientifically testable as written? Explain briefly.

Answer: Not as written. “Happier” is not defined as an observable/measurable outcome.

Problem 14

A student claims a longer pendulum swings more slowly. What measurement would you need to test the claim fairly?

Answer: Measure pendulum length and the time for a fixed number of swings while controlling other conditions.

Problem 17

The relationship average speed = distance / time is used for a toy truck. What does each quantity represent physically?

Relationship: average speed = distance / time

Answer: Average speed describes how much distance is covered per unit time; distance is the path length traveled; time is the elapsed duration.

Problem 18

A model predicts that a longer pendulum has a longer period. A measured period is 1.6 s. Separate the measurement from the interpretation.

Answer: The 1.6 s period is a measurement; the statement that length causes the longer period is a model-based interpretation.

Problem 19

Compare these claims: “A metal rod expands measurably when its temperature increases.” and “The rod expands because warmth makes matter feel relaxed.” Which is more scientifically testable as written, and why?

Answer: The first claim is more testable because it identifies quantities that can be observed or measured. The second uses vague/non-operational language.

Problem 20

A model predicts a steeper ramp should increase the speed of a cart at the bottom. Name one measurement that would support or challenge this prediction.

Answer: Measure cart speed at the bottom for several ramp heights under controlled conditions and compare the results.

Problem 21

A small cart travels 12 m in 4 s. Using average speed = distance/time, determine its average speed and interpret the result.

Relationship: average speed = distance / time

Answer: 3 m/s. The cart covers an average of 3 meters each second.

Problem 22

A robot moves with an average speed of 1.8 m/s for 7 s. Using average speed = distance/time, determine the distance traveled.

Relationship: average speed = distance / time

Answer: distance = speed × time = 1.8 × 7 = 12.6 m.

Problem 24

A toy vehicle travels 15 m in 6 s at a steady average rate. First determine that rate, then use it to predict how far it travels in 10 s.

Relationship: average speed = distance / time

Answer: Rate = 15/6 = 2.5 m/s. In 10 s it travels 2.5×10 = 25 m.
