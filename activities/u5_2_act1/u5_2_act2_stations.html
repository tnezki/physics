Question 1

1. What is constructive interference?

A. Overlap that reinforces displacement
B. Overlap that always gives zero displacement
C. Bending caused by speed change
D. A change in observed frequency
Answer: A — Overlap that reinforces displacement
Work / rationale: Constructive interference occurs when overlapping displacements reinforce and increase the resultant magnitude.

Question 2

2. What is destructive interference?

A. Overlap that always doubles amplitude
B. Overlap that reduces the resultant displacement
C. Reflection from a boundary
D. A natural-frequency response
Answer: B — Overlap that reduces the resultant displacement
Work / rationale: Destructive interference occurs when overlapping displacements oppose and reduce the resultant.

Question 3

3. A fixed point of minimal motion in a standing wave is a

A. antinode
B. crest
C. node
D. compression
Answer: C — node
Work / rationale: A node is a fixed location of minimal displacement in a standing wave.
