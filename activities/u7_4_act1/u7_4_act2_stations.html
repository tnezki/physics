Question 1

1. Electric power measures

A. energy transfer rate
B. charge stored
C. resistance per time
D. energy per charge only
Answer: A — energy transfer rate
Work / rationale: Power is the rate at which energy is transferred or transformed.

Question 2

2. The SI unit of power is the

A. joule-hour
B. ohm
C. watt
D. ampere-hour
Answer: C — watt
Work / rationale: The SI unit of power is the watt, equal to one joule per second.

Question 3

3. Electrical energy use at constant power can be calculated with

A. $E=P/t$
B. $E=Pt$
C. $E=IV/t$
D. $E=IR$
Answer: B — $E=Pt$
Work / rationale: At constant power, energy use is $E=Pt$.

Question 20

20. A panel rated at 300 W produces 150 W for two hours because conditions are weak. Which statement is best?

A. It must still produce 600 Wh because of the rating.
B. The actual energy in that interval is based on the actual power and time, not the 300 W nameplate alone.
C. Power and energy are identical.
D. Weak light cannot change power.
Answer: B — The actual energy in that interval is based on the actual power and time, not the 300 W nameplate alone.
Work / rationale: Actual energy is based on actual power: $(150\text{ W})(2\text{ h})=300\text{ Wh}$. The 300 W rating alone does not determine the interval’s energy.
