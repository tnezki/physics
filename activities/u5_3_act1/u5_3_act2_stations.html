Question 1

1. What must a sound source do?

A. Vibrate
B. Remain perfectly still
C. Create a vacuum
D. Move every medium particle to the listener
Answer: A — Vibrate
Work / rationale: Sound sources must vibrate to create the changing pressure/disturbance that propagates through a medium.

Question 2

2. A high-density region in a longitudinal sound wave is a

A. rarefaction
B. compression
C. crest
D. node only
Answer: B — compression
Work / rationale: A compression is the high-density/high-pressure region of a longitudinal sound wave.

Question 3

3. Sound is called mechanical because it

A. can travel through a perfect vacuum
B. is always transverse
C. requires matter to transmit the disturbance
D. has no energy
Answer: C — requires matter to transmit the disturbance
Work / rationale: Sound is mechanical because it requires matter whose particles interact to transmit the disturbance.
