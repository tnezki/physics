Unit 5.3 · Activity 1 · Nature of SoundSolution 12
Solution 12
DOK 3

12. A claim says sound can cross interplanetary space because radio signals can. Explain why this comparison confuses mechanical sound waves with a different kind of wave.

Rationale: Ordinary sound is a mechanical wave and needs matter to transmit particle-to-particle motion, so it cannot cross a vacuum directly. Radio waves are electromagnetic waves and can propagate through vacuum; they are a different wave type.
