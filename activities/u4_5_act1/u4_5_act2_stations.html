Question 1

1. An energy source is

Answer: A — a resource or process from which usable energy is obtained

Question 2

A. violates conservation
B. It stores or transports energy supplied from a source

D. is identical to a renewable source
Answer: B — It stores or transports energy supplied from a source
Work / rationale: A carrier stores or transports energy supplied from a source; it does not create energy.

Question 3

3. Efficiency describes

A. whether energy is conserved
B. momentum per unit time
C. the fraction or proportion of input becoming useful output for the task
D. mass per unit height
Answer: C — the fraction or proportion of input becoming useful output for the task
Work / rationale: Efficiency is the fraction/proportion of input energy that becomes useful output for the intended task.

Question 19

19. Why does regenerative braking not make a car self-power forever?

A. It creates more energy than the car had.
B. It eliminates thermal energy entirely.
C. It recovers some existing kinetic energy during slowing and still has losses.
D. It works only because momentum is not conserved.
Answer: C — It recovers some existing kinetic energy during slowing and still has losses.
Work / rationale: Regenerative braking recovers some of the car's existing kinetic energy during slowing, but conversion/storage losses remain, so it cannot power the car forever.

Question 20

20. A strong source comparison should

A. use one criterion only

Answer: D — state criteria, evidence, context, and tradeoffs
Work / rationale: A strong comparison states criteria, evidence, local context, and tradeoffs rather than relying on one criterion or a universal answer.
