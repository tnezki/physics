Question 1

1. What produces the magnetic field around a current-carrying wire?

  1. sound waves only
  2. moving electric charges
  3. stationary neutral matter
  4. gravity
Answer: B — moving electric charges
Work / rationale: Electric current is moving electric charge, and moving charge produces a magnetic field.

Question 2

2. The magnetic field around a long straight wire is modeled as

  1. radial lines ending on the wire
  2. no field outside the conductor
  3. lines only along the wire
  4. circles centered on the wire
Answer: D — circles centered on the wire
Work / rationale: A long straight current-carrying wire has magnetic-field directions arranged in circles around the wire.

Question 3

3. A compass near a wire is most useful for detecting

  1. the local magnetic-field direction
  2. wire mass
  3. electric power directly
  4. wire temperature only
Answer: A — the local magnetic-field direction
Work / rationale: A compass needle turns to align with the local magnetic field, making it a direction detector.
