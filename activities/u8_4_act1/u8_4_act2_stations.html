Question 1

1. Electromagnetic induction is the production of voltage when

  1. a north pole is isolated
  2. magnetic conditions through a conductor change
  3. current becomes mass
  4. a magnet and coil remain completely unchanged
Answer: B — magnetic conditions through a conductor change
Work / rationale: Electromagnetic induction occurs when magnetic conditions through a conductor change.

Question 2

2. A generator’s main energy conversion is

  1. thermal to gravitational
  2. magnetic to unlimited energy
  3. electrical to mechanical
  4. mechanical to electrical
Answer: D — mechanical to electrical
Work / rationale: A generator converts supplied mechanical energy into electrical energy; the magnetic field enables the conversion.

Question 3

3. What does alternating current do?

  1. reverses direction repeatedly
  2. keeps voltage permanently positive
  3. moves in one direction only
  4. contains no moving charge
Answer: A — reverses direction repeatedly
Work / rationale: Alternating current repeatedly reverses direction rather than staying one-way.
