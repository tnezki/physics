Station 1 Answers

Question 1

1. Which statement is true for an object moving at constant velocity?

A. Its net force is zero.
B. A forward net force must act.
C. Its acceleration matches its speed.
D. Its mass must be zero.
Answer: A — Its net force is zero.
Work / rationale: Constant velocity means $a=0$, so $F_{net}=ma=0$.

Question 2

2. A cart has 7 N left and 19 N right acting horizontally. Which way does it accelerate?

A. left
B. right
C. it has zero acceleration
D. the direction cannot be found from the forces
Answer: B — right
Work / rationale: $19\text{ N right}-7\text{ N left}=12\text{ N right}$, so acceleration points right.

Question 3

3. If net force is doubled while mass stays fixed, acceleration is

A. halved
B. unchanged
C. doubled
D. made zero
Answer: C — doubled
Work / rationale: With mass fixed, $a=F_{net}/m$; doubling net force doubles acceleration.

Question 11

11. A student claims a moving ball must have a forward net force. Which evidence best refutes the claim?

A. A ball has mass.
B. A ball can be at rest.

D. A larger force can cause larger acceleration.
Answer: C — A ball can move at constant velocity while the net force is zero.
Work / rationale: An object can move at constant velocity while $F_{net}=0$, directly refuting the idea that motion requires a forward net force.

Question 12

Answer: D — For equal mass, larger net force produces larger acceleration.
Work / rationale: For equal masses, $a=F/m$, so the cart with the larger net force has the larger acceleration and changes velocity faster.
