1. After a ball leaves a launcher and air resistance is ignored, why is it considered a projectile?

Answer / rationale: After launch, the object is no longer supported or pushed by the launcher; with air resistance neglected, gravity is the only significant force, so it is a projectile.

Unit 3.5 · Activity 1 · Projectile MotionSolution 2
Solution 2
DOK 1

2. Which component of ideal projectile velocity is unaffected by gravity?

Answer: The horizontal component of velocity is unaffected by gravity in the ideal projectile model. Rationale: With air resistance neglected, gravity acts vertically, so the horizontal net force and horizontal acceleration are zero.

Answer: Downward. Rationale: At the top, vertical velocity is momentarily zero, but gravity still acts, so vertical acceleration remains about $g$ downward.

Unit 3.5 · Activity 1 · Projectile MotionSolution 5
Solution 5
DOK 1

5. What does it mean to say horizontal and vertical components are independent?

Rationale: Independence means horizontal motion does not determine the vertical acceleration or fall time, and vertical motion does not change the ideal horizontal velocity. Both components occur simultaneously over the same time.

11. A student says the projectile is momentarily at rest at the top because vertical velocity is zero. Explain what component the student forgot and state the acceleration there.

Rationale: The student forgot the horizontal velocity component. At the top, only $v_y=0$; $v_x$ remains nonzero, so the projectile is still moving. Its acceleration is still downward at about $g$.

Unit 3.5 · Activity 1 · Projectile MotionSolution 12
Solution 12
DOK 3

12. A drawn path becomes horizontal after the highest point. Critique the path by using the continuing downward acceleration and constant horizontal velocity.

Rationale: After the highest point, gravity continues to produce downward acceleration, so the vertical component becomes increasingly downward while horizontal velocity remains constant. The path must curve downward rather than become horizontal.
