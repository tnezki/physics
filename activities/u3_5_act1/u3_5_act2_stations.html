Question 1

1. After launch, an ideal projectile is influenced significantly by

A. gravity
B. a continuing forward launch force
C. support force
D. surface friction
Answer: A — gravity
Work / rationale: After launch, an ideal projectile is influenced significantly by gravity rather than a continuing launcher force.

Question 2

2. With drag neglected, how does the horizontal component of a projectile's velocity behave?

A. increasing
B. constant
C. decreasing
D. zero
Answer: B — constant
Work / rationale: With no horizontal net force in the ideal model, horizontal acceleration is zero, so $v_x$ remains constant.

Question 3

3. The vertical acceleration of an ideal projectile is

A. upward on the way up
B. zero at the top
C. downward
D. along the path
Answer: C — downward
Work / rationale: Gravity provides a constant downward vertical acceleration.

Work / rationale: $x=v_xt=(8\text{ m/s})(3\text{ s})=24\text{ m}$.

A. 10 m

Answer: C — 20 m
Work / rationale: $y=\tfrac12gt^2=\tfrac12(10)(2^2)=20\text{ m}$.
