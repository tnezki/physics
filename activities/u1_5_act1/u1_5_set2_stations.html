Problem 1

A flawed force model for a hanging sign shows only weight downward. What evidence shows the model is incomplete?

Solution: Because the sign remains at rest, an upward tension force must also act.
Problem 2

Revise this explanation using net force: “The box slows down because friction is present.”

Solution: The box slows because the net force is opposite its velocity; friction contributes to that net force.

Set 2 — Station 5 Solutions

Problem 3

a sled is pulled left while friction acts right. Describe the chain from real situation to force model to physical interpretation.

Solution: Choose the moving object as the system, represent the 12 N driving force left and 5 N opposing force right (plus vertical forces when relevant), then interpret the nonzero net force as a velocity change toward left.
Problem 4

A dog pulls a sled across snow. What should you decide before drawing a force model, and why?

Solution: Choose the system/object first (for example, the sled). That choice determines which interactions count as forces on the object and prevents including forces acting on other objects.
