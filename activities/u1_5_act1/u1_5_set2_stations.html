Station 1 Answers

Question 1

In the equation $C=15+3m$, what could $m$ represent if $C$ is cost?

Solution: $m$ could represent the number of miles, months, minutes, or other units being charged at \$3 each. The $15$ is the starting cost.

Question 2

The graph shows two cost models. Estimate the value of $x$ where the costs are equal, then write the equation that the intersection represents.

Solution: The costs are equal at about $x=5$, with cost about $25$. The intersection represents $15+2x=5+4x$.

Question 3

Construct a strategy for translating a context into an equation before solving. Apply your strategy to: a phone plan charges \$12 plus \$5 per gigabyte and the total bill is \$47.

Solution: Define the variable first: let $g$ be gigabytes. Translate the fixed charge and rate: $12+5g=47$. Solve: $5g=35$, so $g=7$. The bill includes $7$ gigabytes.

Question 4

Design a one-variable equation model for a realistic school or club situation where the solution is $8$. Include the context, define the variable, write the equation, solve it, and interpret why $8$ makes sense.

Solution: One valid model: A club has $4$ posters and prints $2$ posters per student helper. If the total is $20$, then $2x+4=20$. Solving gives $2x=16$, so $x=8$. The solution means $8$ helpers are needed.

Question 5

Solve $5-(2x-3)=-8+2x$. Show the distribution of the negative sign.

Solution: Distribute the negative sign: $5-(2x-3)=5-2x+3=8-2x$. Then $8-2x=-8+2x$, so $16=4x$ and $x=4$.

Question 6

First simplify both sides of $10-2(2x+1)=4(x-2)$, then solve the resulting linear equation.

Solution: Left side: $10-2(2x+1)=10-4x-2=8-4x$. Right side: $4(x-2)=4x-8$. Solve $8-4x=4x-8$, so $16=8x$ and $x=2$.

Question 7

Identify the first operation you would undo in $\frac{x-4}{3}=7$, then solve.

Solution: Undo division by $3$ first: multiply both sides by $3$ to get $x-4=21$. Then add $4$, so $x=25$.

Question 8

A student says $-2\frac{1}{3}+5\frac{1}{6}=3\frac{2}{3}$. Recompute the sum and identify the step where the likely error occurred.

Solution: $-2\frac{1}{3}+5\frac{1}{6}=-\frac{14}{6}+\frac{31}{6}=\frac{17}{6}=2\frac{5}{6}$. The likely error was combining fractional parts without correct common denominators.

Question 9

Start at $-2.5$ on the number line. Move right $3.75$ units and then left $1.25$ units. Mark the pathway and state the final location.

Solution: Start at $-2.5$. Right $3.75$ gives $1.25$. Left $1.25$ gives $0$. The final location is $0$.

Question 10

Complete the diamond problem. The top is the product and the bottom is the sum. Explain which operation you used first.

Solution: The two numbers are $6$ and $-4$ because $6(-4)=-24$ and $6+(-4)=2$.

Question 11

A student solves $3x+5=20$ by subtracting $3$ first and then dividing by $5$, giving $x=\frac{17}{5}$. Critique the pathway and solve correctly.

Solution: Subtracting $3$ first is incorrect because $3x$ is a term, not a number being added. Correct: $3x+5=20$, subtract $5$ to get $3x=15$, then divide by $3$ to get $x=5$.

Question 12

A lab report states: "The sample changed by $-1.2$, then by $-\frac{3}{5}$, so the total change is $-1.7$ because $\frac{3}{5}=0.5$." Revise the model and write the corrected total change with units.

Solution: $\frac{3}{5}=0.6$, so the corrected total change is $-1.2-0.6=-1.8$ units. The report should revise $0.5$ to $0.6$.

Station 4 Answers

Question 13

In the equation $y={5}x+2$, identify the rate of change.

Solution: The rate of change is $5$.

Question 14

A pattern doubles the input and then adds ${3}$. Another pattern adds ${3}$ and then doubles. Compare the outputs when the input is ${5}$.

Solution: Double then add $3$: $2(5)+3=13$. Add $3$ then double: $2(5+3)=16$. The outputs are different because the operations happen in a different order.

Question 15

A student says Plan A is always better because it starts higher. Plan A is $A(x)=x+20$ and Plan B is $B(x)={5}x+4$. Decide whether the claim is reasonable and justify your conclusion.

Solution: The claim is not always reasonable. Compare $A(x)=x+20$ and $B(x)=5x+4$. They are equal when $x+20=5x+4$, so $16=4x$ and $x=4$. Plan A is better for $x\lt4$, they tie at $4$, and Plan B is better after that.

Question 16

Two clubs offer reward plans. Plan A has a larger starting bonus; Plan B earns points faster. Recommend a plan for a student who will participate for ${3}$ weeks and for a student who will participate for ${12}$ weeks. Create or choose equations, then justify using at least two representations.

Solution: One valid comparison: Plan A $A(w)=20+2w$ and Plan B $B(w)=5+5w$. For $3$ weeks, $A(3)=26$ and $B(3)=20$, so choose A. For $12$ weeks, $A(12)=44$ and $B(12)=65$, so choose B. The equations and table values show the larger start versus faster rate.
