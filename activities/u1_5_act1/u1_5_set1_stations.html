Set 1 — Station 5 Solutions

Problem 1

A flawed force model for a resting laptop shows only weight downward. What evidence shows the model is incomplete?

Solution: The unchanged motion indicates an upward support force must also act.
Problem 2

Revise this explanation using net force: “The cart speeds up left because a left force exists.”

Solution: The cart speeds up left because the total leftward force exceeds the total rightward force, producing a net force left.

Set 1 — Station 5 Solutions

Problem 3

a grocery cart is pushed right while rolling resistance acts left. Describe the chain from real situation to force model to physical interpretation.

Solution: Choose the moving object as the system, represent the 14 N driving force right and 6 N opposing force left (plus vertical forces when relevant), then interpret the nonzero net force as a velocity change toward right.
Problem 4

A person pulls a suitcase up a ramp. What should you decide before drawing a force model, and why?

Solution: Choose the system/object first (for example, the suitcase). That choice determines which interactions count as forces on the object and prevents including forces acting on other objects.
