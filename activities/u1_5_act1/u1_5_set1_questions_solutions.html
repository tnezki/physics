A concert ticket costs \$18 plus a \$4 service fee. A student writes $18+4t=90$ to find the number of tickets bought with \$90. Decide whether the model is reasonable and revise it if needed.

Solution: The model is not reasonable if $t$ is the number of tickets, because the \$4 fee is one-time. The revised model is $18t+4=90$. Then $18t=86$, so $t=\frac{86}{18}$, not a whole number; with \$90, the student could buy $4$ tickets and have \$14 left.

Solution 16
Last · DOK 3

Construct a strategy for comparing a graph that passes through $({0},{6})$ and $({4},{18})$ with a table that shows $x$-values ${0}$, ${2}$, ${4}$ and outputs ${10}$, ${16}$, ${22}$. Your strategy must account for starting value, rate of change, and at least one common input.

Solution: Graph: starts at $6$ and has rate $\frac{18-6}{4-0}=3$. Table: starts at $10$ and has rate $3$ because outputs rise by $6$ when $x$ rises by $2$. At $x=4$, graph output is $18$ and table output is $22$, so they have the same rate but different starting values.
