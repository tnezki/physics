Problem 13

A force diagram for the cart includes “person feels cart push backward” as a force on the cart. Diagnose the system error.

Answer: That interaction statement describes a force on another object, not necessarily on the chosen cart. A force diagram must include only forces acting on the chosen system.

Problem 14

Classify the horizontal force model as balanced or unbalanced.

Answer: Balanced; net horizontal force is 0 N.

Problem 15

Rank the three force models by net-force magnitude, greatest to least.

Answer: A=0 N, B=8 N, C=6 N; ranking B > C > A.

Problem 16

A cart has the force model shown while it is described as moving left at constant velocity. Are the force model and motion description consistent?

Answer: No. The net force is 9 N right, so velocity must change rather than remain constant.

Problem 17

A flawed force model for a resting laptop shows only weight downward. What evidence shows the model is incomplete?

Answer: The unchanged motion indicates an upward support force must also act.

Problem 18

Revise this explanation using net force: “The cart speeds up left because a left force exists.”

Answer: The cart speeds up left because the total leftward force exceeds the total rightward force, producing a net force left.

Problem 19

a grocery cart is pushed right while rolling resistance acts left. Describe the chain from real situation to force model to physical interpretation.

Answer: Choose the moving object as the system, represent the 14 N driving force right and 6 N opposing force left (plus vertical forces when relevant), then interpret the nonzero net force as a velocity change toward right.

Problem 20

A person pulls a suitcase up a ramp. What should you decide before drawing a force model, and why?

Answer: Choose the system/object first (for example, the suitcase). That choice determines which interactions count as forces on the object and prevents including forces acting on other objects.

Problem 21

After selecting the object as the system, use the force model to calculate whether the horizontal forces balance.

Answer: Net force = 11 N right, so the forces are unbalanced.

Problem 22

A box moves straight at constant velocity. The model shows 9 N left. What force must be present right for horizontal balance?

Answer: 9 N right.

Problem 23

Complete the quantitative force model by finding the horizontal net force.

Answer: 13 N right.

Problem 24

A wagon model has 21 N right and 13 N left. First determine the net force. Then decide whether a claim of “constant velocity” is consistent with the model.

Answer: Net force = 8 N right. Constant velocity is not consistent because a nonzero net force changes velocity.
