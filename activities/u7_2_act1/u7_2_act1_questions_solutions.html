Unit 7.2 · Activity 1 · Electric Potential and CurrentSolution 10
Solution 10
DOK 2

10. A conducting loop is complete but has no battery, generator, or other source. Explain why a sustained current is not expected.

Answer / rationale: A complete wire loop by itself does not maintain a potential difference. Without a battery, generator, or other source establishing an electric field and supplying energy, any brief charge redistribution dies out rather than producing sustained current.
