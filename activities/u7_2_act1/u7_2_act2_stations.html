Question 1

1. Voltage measures

A. charge per time
B. net charge sign
C. energy per charge
D. electric force per distance
Answer: C — energy per charge
Work / rationale: Voltage is electric potential difference, defined as energy transferred per unit charge, $V=E/Q$.

Question 2

2. Current measures

A. charge flow rate
B. energy per charge
C. stored voltage
D. electric force per time
Answer: A — charge flow rate
Work / rationale: Current is the rate of charge flow, $I=Q/t$.

Question 3

3. Which unit is used for current?

A. volt
B. coulomb
C. joule total
D. ampere
Answer: D — ampere
Work / rationale: The SI unit of current is the ampere (A).

Question 9

A. half as large
B. unchanged
C. four times as large
D. twice as large
Answer: D — twice as large
Work / rationale: $E=qV$. At the same voltage, doubling the charge doubles the total energy transferred.

Question 10

10. An open switch is inserted into the only loop. Which outcome is expected?

A. Voltage everywhere must become zero.
B. Sustained current stops because the conducting path is incomplete.
C. Charge is destroyed at the switch.
D. Every charge in the wire becomes neutral.
Answer: B — Sustained current stops because the conducting path is incomplete.
Work / rationale: Opening the only loop breaks the conducting path, so sustained current stops.
