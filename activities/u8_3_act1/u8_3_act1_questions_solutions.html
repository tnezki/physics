1. How does a current-carrying coil differ from a permanent magnet in how its magnetism is produced?

Answer: A current-carrying coil produces controllable magnetism from electric current; a permanent magnet retains magnetization because of persistent domain alignment without needing circuit current.

11. A crane operator can release scrap metal by switching off an electromagnet. Explain the behavior using current-produced field and iron-core/domain ideas rather than saying the magnet “runs out.”

Answer: Switching off current collapses the coil’s controllable magnetic field. The soft-iron core’s domains are no longer strongly aligned by that field, so the lifting attraction drops and the scrap can be released.
