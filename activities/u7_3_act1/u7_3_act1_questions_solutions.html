12. A device heats during operation and its $V$–$I$ graph becomes progressively steeper. Explain how temperature-dependent resistance could account for the pattern.

Answer / rationale: On a $V$-versus-$I$ graph, slope is $V/I=R$. A progressively steeper graph means the effective resistance is increasing. Heating a filament can raise its resistance, so each additional ampere requires more voltage.
