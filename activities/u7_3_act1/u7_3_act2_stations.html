Station 1 Answers

Question 1

1. Electrical resistance is measured in

A. amperes
B. volts
C. coulombs
D. ohms
Answer: D — ohms
Work / rationale: Electrical resistance is measured in ohms ($\Omega$).

Question 2

2. Which wire has less resistance if material and length are the same?

A. the thinner wire
B. the thicker wire
C. both always have infinite resistance
D. thickness never matters
Answer: B — the thicker wire
Work / rationale: For the same material and length, a thicker wire has greater cross-sectional area and therefore lower resistance.

Question 3

3. Ohm’s Law is

A. $V=IR$
B. $V=E/q$
C. $I=Q/t$
D. $F=kq_1q_2/r^2$
Answer: A — $V=IR$
Work / rationale: Ohm’s Law relates voltage, current, and resistance as $V=IR$.

Question 19

Question 20

20. A bird touches only one ideal high-voltage wire with both feet close together. Which explanation best addresses why current through the bird can be small?

A. Birds have zero resistance.
B. There is little potential difference across its body, so Ohm’s Law does not imply a large body current from the wire’s voltage label alone.
C. High voltage cannot produce current anywhere.
D. The bird stores the wire voltage as charge.
Answer: B — There is little potential difference across its body, so Ohm’s Law does not imply a large body current from the wire’s voltage label alone.
Work / rationale: The bird can be at high electric potential while the potential difference across its nearby feet/body is small; a small $\Delta V$ produces a small body current for finite resistance.
