Unit 7.1 · Activity 1 · Electric Charge and ForcesSolution 7
Solution 7
DOK 2

7. The force between two charged particles is 18 N. Their distance is doubled with charge amounts unchanged. Determine the new force.

Work: Coulomb force varies as $1/r^2$. Doubling distance gives $F_{new}=18/(2^2)=18/4=4.5\text{ N}$. Answer: $4.5\text{ N}$.

10. A grounded conductor is near a charged object. Explain how the Earth connection can allow electrons to enter or leave the conductor during induction.

Answer / rationale: Attraction alone does not prove opposite net charges because a charged object can polarize a neutral conductor, bringing opposite charge slightly closer and producing net attraction.
