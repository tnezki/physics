Question 1

1. Refraction occurs when light

A. changes speed and usually direction as it enters a different medium
B. returns from a mirror without crossing
C. forms a shadow only
D. changes into sound
Answer: A — changes speed and usually direction as it enters a different medium
Work / rationale: Refraction occurs when light crosses into a different medium, changes speed, and usually changes direction.

Question 2

2. Entering a slower medium at an angle, light bends

A. away from the normal
B. along the surface
C. toward the normal
D. back along the incident path
Answer: C — toward the normal
Work / rationale: Entering a slower/higher-index medium makes an oblique ray bend toward the normal.

Question 3

3. An apparent-depth effect is caused mainly by

A. reflection only
B. refraction
C. absorption
D. shadow formation
Answer: B — refraction
Work / rationale: Apparent-depth shifts arise when refracted rays are back-traced to a false straight-line location.

Question 6

6. Light speed is $2.0\times10^8$ m/s in a medium. Its index is

A. 1.5
B. 0.67
C. 2.0
D. 6.0
Answer: A — 1.5
Work / rationale: $n=c/v=(3.0\times10^8)/(2.0\times10^8)=1.5$.

Question 7

7. A ray goes from air into glass. If it slows, the refracted angle should usually be

A. larger than the incident angle
B. exactly 90°
C. unrelated to the normal
D. smaller than the incident angle
Answer: D — smaller than the incident angle
Work / rationale: Slowing in glass means a larger refractive index, so Snell’s law gives a smaller refracted angle than the incident angle.
