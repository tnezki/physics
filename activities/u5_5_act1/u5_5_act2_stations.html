Question 1

1. Two same-phase sound waves at a point produce

A. constructive interference
B. destructive interference
C. refraction
D. Doppler effect
Answer: A — constructive interference
Work / rationale: Same-phase pressure variations add constructively and increase the resultant amplitude.

Question 2

2. Two equal opposite-phase sound waves at a point can produce

A. constructive interference only
B. destructive interference
C. resonance only
D. echo only
Answer: B — destructive interference
Work / rationale: Equal opposite-phase contributions can cancel through destructive interference.

Question 3

3. A repeating loud-soft pattern from close tones is called

A. refraction
B. a compression
C. beats
D. an echo
Answer: C — beats
Work / rationale: Two close frequencies produce a repeating loud-soft beat pattern.

Question 17

17. If a cancellation tone does not match a changing noise frequency, cancellation usually

A. becomes less effective
B. becomes perfect
C. stops requiring phase matching
D. turns into refraction
Answer: A — becomes less effective
Work / rationale: If the cancellation signal no longer matches the changing noise frequency, phase opposition cannot be maintained well and cancellation becomes less effective.

Question 18

A. The source frequencies randomly turn on and off.

Answer: B — Changing path difference changes the relative phase and interference.
Work / rationale: As the listener moves, path difference changes the relative phase of the two signals, creating alternating constructive and destructive interference.
