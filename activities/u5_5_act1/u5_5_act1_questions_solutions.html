Rationale: Strong cancellation requires the canceling signal to match both the incoming frequency content and its phase/timing. A rapidly changing sound changes too quickly for one fixed opposite waveform to stay matched.

Solution 12
DOK 3

12. A model of noise cancellation shows an “opposite” wave but does not specify timing. Explain why the model is incomplete and what phase information is needed.

Rationale: Saying only “opposite” is insufficient. For strong cancellation, the canceling waveform must arrive approximately $180^\circ$ out of phase with the unwanted sound at the listener and must track its timing/frequency so the opposing pressure variations coincide.
