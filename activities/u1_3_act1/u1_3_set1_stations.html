Station 1 Answers

Question 1

The equation $y=2|x-5|+1$ belongs to which common family?

Solution: It belongs to the absolute value family because the equation contains $|x-5|$.

Question 2

For exponential growth, is the multiplier usually greater than $1$ or between $0$ and $1$?

Solution: For exponential growth, the multiplier is usually greater than $1$.

Question 3

Use equation structure to identify the family and one key feature for $y=-2|x+3|+5$.

Solution: The equation is absolute value. The vertex is $(-3,5)$ because $x+3=x-(-3)$ and the outside value is $5$. The negative coefficient means the graph opens downward.

Question 4

Two students classify the table. Student A checks first differences; Student B checks ratios. Use both methods to decide the family and explain which method gives clearer evidence here.

$x$0123
$y$481632
Solution: First differences are $4,8,16$, not constant. Ratios are $2,2,2$, which are constant. The table is exponential, and the ratio method gives clearer evidence.

Station 2 Answers

Question 5

Compare $-5^2$ and $(-5)^2$. Evaluate both and explain the role of parentheses in one sentence.

Solution: $-5^2=-(5^2)=-25$, while $(-5)^2=25$. Parentheses make the negative part of the base before squaring.

Question 6

Complete the substitution table for $2n^2-3$.

$n$$-2$$0$$3$
$2n^2-3$
Solution: For $n=-2$, $2(-2)^2-3=8-3=5$. For $n=0$, $-3$. For $n=3$, $2(9)-3=15$. The completed outputs are $5,-3,15$.

Question 7

An expression has $6x$ terms, $-2x$ terms, and $9$ unit terms. Use algebraic notation to represent and simplify it.

Solution: $6x+(-2x)+9=4x+9$.

Question 8

Choose the more useful form for mental calculation when $x=25$: $4x+20$ or $4(x+5)$. Evaluate and justify your choice.

Solution: $4(x+5)$ is more useful mentally: $4(25+5)=4(30)=120$. The form $4x+20$ also gives $100+20=120$.

Question 9

Build an expression equivalent to $10y-15$ that has exactly two terms inside parentheses and a common factor outside. Explain your structure.

Solution: One equivalent expression is $5(2y-3)$. It has common factor $5$ outside and two terms, $2y$ and $-3$, inside parentheses.

Question 10

Construct a strategy for solving and graphing $-3(x-2)\ge 15$. Your strategy must address distribution, division by a negative, and the number-line boundary.

Solution: Solve: $-3(x-2)\ge15$. Divide by $-3$ and reverse the inequality: $x-2\le-5$. Add $2$: $x\le-3$. On the number line, use a closed dot at $-3$ and shade left.

Question 11

Solve $4-2x\lt 10$ using two methods: first by inverse operations and then by testing values around the boundary. Explain how the methods support the same solution set.

Solution: Inverse operations: $4-2x\lt10$ gives $-2x\lt6$, so $x\gt-3$ after dividing by $-2$ and reversing the sign. Testing $x=-4$ gives false and $x=0$ gives true, supporting $x\gt-3$.

Question 12

A context involves $12$ identical groups of $x+4$ objects. Recommend two equivalent expression forms and explain which form is better for seeing the number of groups and which is better for calculating a total.

Solution: Two forms are $12(x+4)$ and $12x+48$. The factored form better shows $12$ identical groups of $x+4$. The expanded form is better for quickly calculating the total after distributing.

Question 15

A line passes through $(-2,{0})$ and $({0},{4})$. Find both intercepts and describe what each intercept tells you about the graph.

Solution: The intercepts are $(-2,0)$ and $(0,4)$. The $x$-intercept is where the graph crosses the horizontal axis; the $y$-intercept is the starting output when $x=0$.

Question 16

A student claims a pattern is linear because the outputs keep getting larger. Use the values ${2}$, ${5}$, ${9}$, ${14}$, ${20}$ to decide whether the claim is reasonable.

Solution: The claim is not reasonable. The differences are $3,4,5,6$, which are not constant. Increasing outputs alone do not prove a linear pattern.
