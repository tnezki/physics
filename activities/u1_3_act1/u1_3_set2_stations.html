Question 1

For exponential decay, is the multiplier usually greater than $1$ or between $0$ and $1$?

Solution: For exponential decay, the multiplier is usually between $0$ and $1$.

Question 2

The graph shows a growth pattern. Estimate whether a linear or exponential family is more reasonable, then support your answer with how the outputs change.

Solution: Exponential is more reasonable. The graph curves upward and the increases get larger as the step increases, rather than staying constant as in a linear model.

Question 3

A coach records jump heights: $1.0,1.4,1.9,2.5,3.2$ feet. A student claims an exponential model is best because the values increase. Is that reasonable? Use approximate differences or ratios as evidence.

Solution: The claim is not very reasonable from increase alone. Differences are about $0.4,0.5,0.6,0.7$, while ratios are not close to constant. A linear or quadratic-type pattern may be more reasonable than exponential evidence here.

Question 4

A school wants to predict club membership for the next four meetings. Approach A uses a constant increase of $6$ students each meeting from $18$. Approach B multiplies by $1.25$ each meeting from $18$. Recommend one approach for meetings $1$ through $4$ if room capacity is $60$. Support your recommendation with a table and family evidence.

Solution: Approach A gives $24,30,36,42$ for meetings $1$–$4$. Approach B gives about $22.5,28.1,35.2,43.9$. Both stay under $60$, but Approach A is simpler and gives similar predictions over four meetings. Choose A if the school wants a clear, conservative linear plan.

Question 5

Simplify each expression by combining like terms.

  1. $y+0.03y$
  2. $z-0.2z$
  3. $x+0.002x$
Solution:
  1. $y+0.03y=1.03y$.
  2. $z-0.2z=0.8z$.
  3. $x+0.002x=1.002x$.

Question 6

Identify the coefficient of each variable term.

  1. $-7x$
  2. $\frac{3}{4}m$
  3. $p$
  4. $-0.6q$
Solution:
  1. The coefficient of $-7x$ is $-7$.
  2. The coefficient of $\frac{3}{4}m$ is $\frac{3}{4}$.
  3. The coefficient of $p$ is $1$.
  4. The coefficient of $-0.6q$ is $-0.6$.

Question 7

Circle the like terms in $5x-3+2x+7-4y+x$. Then combine only the like terms.

Solution: The like terms are $5x,2x,$ and $x$, and the constants are $-3$ and $7$. Combine to get $8x+4-4y$. The $-4y$ is not like the $x$ terms.

Question 8

A rectangle has perimeter $46$ inches and width $8$ inches. Use $2l+2w=P$ to write and solve an equation for the length.

Solution: Use $2l+2(8)=46$. Then $2l+16=46$, $2l=30$, and $l=15$. The length is $15$ inches.

Question 9

Solve $3(2x-5)+4=25$ and check by substituting into the original equation.

Solution: $3(2x-5)+4=25$ gives $6x-15+4=25$, so $6x-11=25$, $6x=36$, and $x=6$. Check: $3(12-5)+4=3(7)+4=25$.

Question 10

A student has $12$ practice cards and adds the same number each day for $5$ days, ending with $47$ cards. Write and solve an equation for the number added each day.

Solution: Let $d$ be cards added each day. $12+5d=47$. Then $5d=35$ and $d=7$. The student adds $7$ cards per day.

Question 11

A student claims $x\le6$ is reasonable for the statement "a container can hold fewer than $6$ liters." Analyze the boundary value and revise the inequality if needed.

Solution: “Fewer than $6$ liters” does not include $6$, so $x\le6$ is too broad. The correct inequality is $x\lt6$.

Question 12

Create a one-variable equation with parentheses whose solution is $x=6$. The equation must require distribution before solving. Show the solution check.

Solution: One valid equation is $2(x+4)=20$. Distribute: $2x+8=20$, so $2x=12$ and $x=6$. Check: $2(6+4)=20$.

Question 16

Create a linear pattern with constraints: Figure ${0}$ has a nonzero number of tiles, Figure ${5}$ has more than ${25}$ tiles, and the growth is not ${1}$, ${2}$, or ${5}$ tiles per figure. Provide a table, rule, and graph.

Solution: One valid pattern: start at $6$ tiles and grow by $4$ each figure. Then Figure $5$ has $6+4(5)=26$ tiles, which is more than $25$. Table: $(0,6),(1,10),(2,14),(3,18),(4,22),(5,26)$. Rule: $y=4x+6$.
