Problem 1

A skateboarder’s velocity changes from 5 m/s east to 20 m/s east in 5 s. Calculate the average acceleration.

  1. 3 m/s² east
  2. 4.0 m/s² east
  3. 5 m/s² east
  4. 15 m/s east
Solution: a = Δv/t = (20 − 5) m/s ÷ 5 s = 3 m/s² east.
