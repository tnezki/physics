Problem 1

A skateboarder’s velocity changes from 5 m/s east to 20 m/s east in 5 s. Calculate the average acceleration.

  1. 3 m/s² east
  2. 4.0 m/s² east
  3. 5 m/s² east
  4. 15 m/s east

Solution: a = Δv/t = (20 − 5) m/s ÷ 5 s = 3 m/s² east.

Problem 2

A scooter speeds up in a straight line. Explain whether it is accelerating and identify what part of velocity is changing.

  1. Yes. It accelerates because the magnitude of velocity increases.
  2. No; acceleration means only speeding up.
  3. No; acceleration requires changing direction and speed at the same time.
  4. Only objects in free fall accelerate.

Solution: Acceleration is any change in velocity: speed, direction, or both.
