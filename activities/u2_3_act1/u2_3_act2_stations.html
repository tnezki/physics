Station 1 Answers

Question 1

1. Acceleration measures the rate of change of

A. velocity
B. mass
C. distance only
D. position only
Answer: A — velocity
Work / rationale: Acceleration measures how quickly velocity changes with time.

Question 2

2. Which motion can have nonzero acceleration?

A. constant straight velocity
B. turning at constant speed
C. remaining at rest
D. unchanged velocity
Answer: B — turning at constant speed
Work / rationale: Turning changes velocity direction, so acceleration can be nonzero even when speed is constant.

Question 3

3. A positive-moving object slows down. Its acceleration is usually

A. positive
B. zero
C. negative
D. undefined
Answer: C — negative
Work / rationale: If positive velocity is getting smaller, $\Delta v$ is negative, so the acceleration is negative.

Question 19

19. Velocity changes from -2 m/s to +4 m/s in 3 s. Average acceleration is

A. -2 m/s²
B. +6 m/s²
C. +2 m/s²
D. 0 m/s²
Answer: C — +2 m/s²
Work / rationale: $a=[4-(-2)]\text{ m/s}/3\text{ s}=6/3=+2\text{ m/s}^2$.

Question 20

20. Why can an object accelerate even when its speedometer reading stays constant?

A. Speed and velocity are identical.
B. Acceleration depends only on mass.
C. Time can be ignored.
D. Velocity can change through direction.
Answer: D — Velocity can change through direction.
Work / rationale: Acceleration depends on velocity, and velocity can change through direction even when the speedometer reading stays constant.
