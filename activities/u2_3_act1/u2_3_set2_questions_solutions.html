Problem 5

Problem 5

A skateboarder’s velocity changes from 6 m/s east to 22 m/s east in 4 s. Calculate the average acceleration.

Solution: a = Δv/t = (22 − 6) m/s ÷ 4 s = 4 m/s² east.
