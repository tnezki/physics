Q1

A cart’s velocity changes from 1 m/s to 9 m/s in 2 s. Calculate its average acceleration.

  1. 8 m/s²
  2. 16 m/s²
  3. 5 m/s²
  4. 4 m/s²
S1

A cart’s velocity changes from 1 m/s to 9 m/s in 2 s. Calculate its average acceleration.

Answer: 4 m/s²
Reasoning: a = Δv/Δt = (9 − 1) m/s / 2 s = 4 m/s².

Q10

S10

A skateboarder moves west while slowing down. State the direction of the skateboarder’s velocity and acceleration, and explain why the skateboarder is accelerating even though speed decreases.

Answer: Velocity is west; acceleration is east. Acceleration is any change in velocity, including a decrease in speed.
Reasoning: To slow an object, acceleration points opposite the velocity.
