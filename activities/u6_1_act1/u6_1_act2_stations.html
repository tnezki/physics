Answer: A — An electromagnetic wave

A. $c=f/\lambda$
B. $c=\lambda/f$
C. $c=f\lambda$
D. $f=c\lambda$
Answer: C — $c=f\lambda$
Work / rationale: The wave relation is $c=f\lambda$.

D. a mechanical vibration
Answer: B — a small portion of the electromagnetic spectrum
Work / rationale: Visible wavelengths occupy only a small band within the full electromagnetic spectrum.
