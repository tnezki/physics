Answer: A — reflection

Question 2

A. the speed of thought
B. the size of the retina
C. the directions of incoming light rays
D. the color of every object
Answer: C — the directions of incoming light rays
Work / rationale: Corrective lenses work by changing incoming ray directions so the eye can focus them on the retina.

Question 3

3. Light in an optical fiber is commonly guided by

A. shadow formation
B. total internal reflection
C. diffuse reflection only
D. continuous absorption
Answer: B — total internal reflection
Work / rationale: Optical fibers commonly guide rays by total internal reflection at the core-cladding boundary.
