Section 2.4 · Free Fall

Practice these ideas in a new situation.

Explain and justify · 2.4

13. A ball is thrown upward and has just left the thrower’s hand. At the very top of its flight, its instant velocity is zero. If air resistance is ignored, is its acceleration zero? Explain the direction and cause.

Calculate and interpret · 2.4

Calculate and interpret · 2.4
