Mastery Goal 2: Newton’s First Law & Inertia

5.

A spacecraft coasts through deep space with negligible net force. Predict what happens next and justify with Newton’s First Law.

6.

Explain why a loaded grocery cart is harder to start or stop than the same empty cart, using the word inertia correctly.

7.

A picture frame hangs motionless with balanced forces. Name the equilibrium state.

8.

A hanging object is in equilibrium. The total upward force is 294 N. Determine the downward weight.

$\sum F_y=0 \Rightarrow F_{\text{up}}=F_{\text{down}}$

Mastery Goal 3: Net Force & Vectors

9.

A student reports “the force is 12 N” and stops. What information is missing from a complete vector description?

10.

A cart has a 22 N force to the right. Its net force is 15 N to the right. Determine the leftward force.

$F_{\text{net}}=F_{\text{right}}-F_{\text{left}}$

11.

Two students push a sled in the same direction with 18 N and 6 N. Determine the net force.

$F_{\text{net}}=F_1+F_2$

12.

Use the supplied vector diagram to read the arrow-direction convention. For a one-dimensional case with 26 N right and 11 N left, calculate the resultant and state its direction.
