How the model calculates its prediction

For constant net force F over a push distance d, it uses v = √(2Fd/m); impulse J = mv and force duration Δt = J/F. For a horizontal release from a 1 m height, the range is v√(2h/g). This predicts trends only; it does not reproduce the real launcher precisely.
