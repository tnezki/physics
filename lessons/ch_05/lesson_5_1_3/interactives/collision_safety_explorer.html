How this model works: It assumes constant average deceleration during the stop and an initial 5 cm effective stopping distance even without foam. The idealized stopping distance is 5 cm + foam thickness, so Δt = 2d / v. These are not measured foam properties. Actual collision forces and foam behavior depend on material stiffness, compression, peak load, and energy loss.
