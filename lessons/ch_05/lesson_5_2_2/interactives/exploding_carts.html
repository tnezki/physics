Our idealized spring produces a fixed 1.20 N·s impulse on each cart in opposite directions. The cart with less mass therefore leaves with more speed.

This is an independently built classroom model, not the publisher's original simulation. It assumes no significant external impulse, begins at rest, and shows a fixed spring impulse for comparing trials.
