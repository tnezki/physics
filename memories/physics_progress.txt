WHAT THIS FILE IS

The progress tracker is a student-facing study guide.
It does two things:
  1. Gives students the learning ladder and assessment ideas
     from the assessment plan — with no clickable links.
  2. Gives students 32 practice problems (8 per DOK level)
     derived from the bank, so they can self-assess their
     readiness and know what kinds of questions to expect.

It is NOT an assessment. It is NOT a formative or summative.
It contains no answers, no scoring rubric, no teacher notes
that shouldn't be in a student document.

PART 2 — 32 PRACTICE QUESTIONS

The second half of the document is new content generated by
reading the bank. It does NOT come from the assessment plan.

GENERAL RULES:
  — 8 questions per DOK level = 32 total.
  — Question numbers restart at 1 for each DOK section.
  — Each question has a .workspace-label and .workspace div.
  — Workspace height scales with DOK complexity (see below).
  — Questions are AI-derived: new problems that exemplify the
    unit's learning targets at each DOK level, informed by
    the vocabulary, skill types, and contexts in the bank.
  — Do not copy bank items verbatim. Write questions that
    demonstrate what the unit's DOK level looks like.
  — Cover the full range of the unit's skill targets.
    Do not write 8 variations of the same problem.
  — Do not reference formative or summative assessments.
  — Do not include answer keys or teacher-only information.

WORKSPACE SIZES BY DOK:
  DOK 1: .workspace-100 or .workspace-120
    (computation or identification — short answer)
  DOK 2: .workspace-140 or .workspace-160
    (multi-step application — moderate workspace)
  DOK 3: .workspace-180 or .workspace-200
    (reasoning, justification, analysis — larger workspace)
  DOK 4: .workspace-240 or .workspace-260
    (design, model, critique — maximum workspace)

  Use the smaller size when the question is brief.
  Use the larger size when the prompt has multiple parts.

QUESTION TYPES BY DOK:
  DOK 1: identify, recall, compute, read a graph/table,
    evaluate a single expression, name a property.
    Multiple choice is allowed for DOK 1 (use <ol class="lower-alpha">).
  DOK 2: calculate and explain, graph and label, write an
    equation from a representation, compare two quantities,
    make a prediction using a routine model.
  DOK 3: justify or defend a claim, analyze an error,
    explain a pattern or structure, compare two strategies,
    interpret a representation in context.
  DOK 4: design a model, create a context with constraints,
    critique and revise a given model, compare two options
    and justify a recommendation, explain limitations.

QUESTION CONTENT PATTERNS:

Plain text question:
  <p>[question prompt]</p>
  <div class="workspace-label">Workspace</div>
  <div class="workspace workspace-[N]"></div>

Multiple choice (DOK 1 only):
  <p>[question prompt]</p>
  <ol class="lower-alpha">
  <li>[option]</li>
  <li>[option]</li>
  <li>[option]</li>
  <li>[option]</li>
  </ol>
  <div class="workspace-label">Workspace</div>
  <div class="workspace workspace-100"></div>

Table question:
  <p>[question prompt]</p>
  <table class="table-block">
  <tr><th>[col header]</th><td>[val]</td><td>[val]</td></tr>
  <tr><th>[col header]</th><td>[val]</td><td>[val]</td></tr>
  </table>
  <div class="workspace-label">Workspace</div>
  <div class="workspace workspace-[N]"></div>

Figure question:
  <p>[question prompt]</p>
  <div class="graph-block graph-md">
    <img src="figures/[filename].png" alt="[non-answer description]">
  </div>
  <div class="workspace-label">Workspace</div>
  <div class="workspace workspace-[N]"></div>

FIGURE NAMING FOR PROGRESS TRACKER:
  u[unit]_pt_[skill]_[num].png
  Examples:
    u0A_pt_numberline_01.png
    u2_pt_scatter_pos_01.png
    u1_pt_blank_grid_01.png
  Shared blank figures (number line, coordinate plane) use
  one file, not numbered duplicates.
