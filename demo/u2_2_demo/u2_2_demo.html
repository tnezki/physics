Same Speed, Different Motion

I will move. You will time, calculate, and explain.

A6 metersB

Get ready

Use a stopwatch or timer and paper / Notability for your data.

Timing rule

Start when I first move. Stop when I reach the finish point.

Big question: Can two trips have about the same average speed, but different motion?

Walk 1 | 2-5 min

Steady walk: A → B

A6 metersB
  1. Everyone times my walk from A to B.
  2. Record your time in row 1.
  3. Calculate your average speed.
6 m ÷ your time = _____ m/s
Question: Does this answer prove I moved at exactly that speed every instant?
Walk 2 | 5-9 min

Different speeds during one trip

Watch: slow → quick → pause → quick.

  1. Everyone times the entire 6 m trip, including the pause.
  2. Record your time in row 2 and calculate average speed.
  3. Compare your answers from walks 1 and 2.
6 m ÷ your time = _____ m/s
At the pause, what was my instantaneous speed? If the two overall times are close, was the motion still different?
Walk 3 | 9-13 min

Go out … then return to A

A → B → A (6 m out + 6 m back)

  1. Time the entire walk until I return to A.
  2. Record your time and calculate average speed using 12 m.
  3. Watch the arrow: what happens to the direction of my velocity?
12 m ÷ your time = _____ m/s
I finish where I started. Is my average speed zero? What is my net change in position?
Walk 4 | 13-15 min | observation only

Same pace, turning direction

I walk on a curve at approximately one steady speed.

Watch the arrow

It always points in the direction I am moving.

No timing needed

Focus on what changes, even if the pace stays about the same.

Prediction: If my speed stays roughly constant but the arrow turns, does my velocity stay constant?

Compare this to the steady, straight middle part of walk 1.

15-18 min | think, discuss, defend

Use evidence from our walks

  1. Walks 1 and 2 may have similar average speeds. How did their instantaneous speeds differ?
  2. In walk 3, why was my average speed greater than zero even though I ended at A? What happened to direction?
  3. During the curved walk, could my speed stay constant while my velocity changed? Explain using the arrow.
Speed = how fast.   Velocity = how fast + which direction.
