Unit 2 - Section 2 - Speed and Velocity

Fast Trip, Zero Direction?

Speed describes how fast; velocity adds direction.

Watch, predict, and be ready to defend what you notice.

The trip

You walk from school to a friend’s house, then return to school.

You definitely moved. But how should the whole trip be described?
Two numbers can tell different stories

Suppose the total trip is 2.0 km and takes 20 min.

Can your average speed be nonzero even though you finish where you started?
Add direction

Compare: “5 m/s” and “5 m/s east.”

Which statement tells more about the motion? What extra information was added?
Constant speed is not always constant motion

Imagine a car moving around a curve while the speedometer stays at 30 mph.

Did the car’s motion stay completely unchanged?
Question to carry into the notes

When does “how fast” describe enough, and when must direction be included?

Keep this question unresolved until the section model gives us better language and evidence.
