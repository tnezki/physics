Unit 6 - Section 6.1 - Nature of Light

The Beam You Cannot See

A laser spot is bright on the target. From the side, the space between source and target looks almost empty. Is light actually traveling through that space?
Make a prediction

Where is the light?

Choose a model before we test it: A. light exists only at the bright spot, or B. light is traveling all the way from source to target.
Test the empty-looking space

Use a card as a detector

Without moving the laser, place a small white card halfway between the source and target. What appears on the card? Move the card to two other places along the path.
Remove the detector

What changed?

The bright spot disappears from the middle when the card is removed, but the original target spot returns. What does that tell you about the path?
Question to carry into the notes

Why do you see some light paths but not others?

Build an explanation that follows light from a source, through space or matter, and finally into an eye.
