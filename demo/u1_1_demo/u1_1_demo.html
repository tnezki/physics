Unit 1 · Section 1.1 · Thinking Like a Physicist

The Hidden Bounce

Your rule: report only what you directly observe. Do not explain it yet.

Your teacher will create an event where you cannot see the object.

Round 1 · Observe

What did you actually observe?

Direct observation

Something a person, camera, microphone, timer, or measuring tool could record.

Inference

An explanation you build from the observations.

Be ready to defend why your statement belongs in one column and not the other.
Round 2 · Model

What could have caused the pattern?

Generate at least two different explanations that fit what you observed.

A good model explains the observations. A scientific model must also face a test.
Round 3 · Test

What new evidence would separate the models?

Propose a test

What could we change, measure, reveal, or repeat?

Make a prediction

If your model is right, what should the new observation look or sound like?

The physics habit

Observation → Inference → Test → Revision

Physics is not about guessing the hidden answer. It is about building explanations that survive contact with evidence.
