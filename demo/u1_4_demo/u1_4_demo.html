The Tension Trap

One mass hangs at rest from two spring scales. Nothing about the mass changes.

Should each scale always read the same amount?

Setup A · Steep supports

Hold the scales high and fairly close together.

The mass is at rest. Record or remember both scale readings.

What must be true about the net force while the mass remains still?

Setup B · Wider supports

Spread the scales farther apart.

The same mass is still at rest. Predict what happens to the scale readings before looking.
