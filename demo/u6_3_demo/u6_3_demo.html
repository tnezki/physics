Unit 6 - Section 6.3 - Refraction and Total Internal Reflection

The Coin That Appears

A coin is on the table behind the rim of an empty cup. From your fixed viewing position, the coin is hidden. Can the coin become visible without moving the coin, cup, or your eyes?
Commit to a prediction

Only one thing will change

Water will be added slowly to the cup. Predict: coin stays hidden, becomes visible, or something else. Explain what you think must happen to the light path.
Observe

Keep your head still

Watch the same location while water is poured in. When does the coin first become visible?
Rule out the easy explanation

What did not move?

The coin, cup, and observer stayed fixed. The new evidence must come from a changed path between the coin and the eye.
Question to carry into the notes

Why did adding water change the path?

What property of light changes when it crosses from one material into another at an angle?
