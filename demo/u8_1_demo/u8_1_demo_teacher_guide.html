Physics - Unit 8 - Section 8.1 - Demo Teacher Guide

Something Is Organizing the Compasses

Hidden-magnet field phenomenon - designed for 5-8 minutes

Time5-8 minutes
FormatCompass field reveal
Core ideaInvisible field with direction

Why This Demo Fits Section 8.1

The section begins with magnetic poles, field lines, field direction/strength, and domains. A hidden magnet plus compasses makes the field model necessary before students have the vocabulary for it.

Materials

  • Bar magnet
  • 4-8 small compasses
  • Sheet of paper or thin cardboard
  • Tape

Before Class

  1. Tape the magnet under the center of the sheet so students cannot see its orientation.
  2. Place compasses at several positions but do not arrange them into a perfect textbook pattern before the reveal.

Suggested Flow

MinutesProjected PhaseTeacher Focus
1Opening predictionAsk whether the needles should be random or organized.
2First observationLet students describe only what the needles do.
1-2Move compassesShow that direction depends on location and is repeatable.
1Model choiceFavor evidence over vocabulary.
1-2BridgeAsk how an invisible directional effect could be represented.

Expected Observations / Evidence

Compasses settle into a repeatable position-dependent pattern. Near the magnet the influence is usually more obvious than far away.

Watch for: Keep other magnets, steel objects, and powered electronics away from the setup. Do not tell students that drawn field lines are physical strings.

Transition Into Notes

The notes formalize magnetic poles, field direction, relative field strength, pole pairs, and magnetic domains.
