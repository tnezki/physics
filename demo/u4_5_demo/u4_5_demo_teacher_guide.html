Physics - Unit 4 - Section 5 - Demo Teacher Guide

There Is No One-Column Winner

Evidence-and-tradeoff hook - designed for 5-8 minutes

Time5-8 minutes
FormatWhole-class decision prompt
Core ideaEnergy decisions require multiple criteria and context

Why This Demo Fits Section 4.5

Section 4.5 asks students to distinguish sources, carriers, and transformations and to compare energy options using availability, efficiency, environmental impact, and reliability. This hook exposes why one-criterion slogans are inadequate.

Materials

  • Projected demo only
  • Optional four corner signs: Solar, Wind, Geothermal, Fossil Fuel

Before Class

  1. If using corners, post the four source labels.
  2. Do not provide a “correct winner”; the point is that rankings depend on evidence and constraints.

Suggested Flow

MinutesProjected PhaseTeacher Focus
0-2Initial choiceStudents choose a source with almost no information.
2-4Add reliabilityAsk whether rankings change.
4-6Add environmental impact + availabilityMake students revise or defend their choice.
6-8BridgeName the need for evidence categories and context.

Expected Observations / Student Thinking

Students will disagree and often change choices as criteria are added. That disagreement is productive because it reveals hidden assumptions about what “best” means.

Watch for: Avoid turning the hook into a political debate or claiming universal rankings. Keep the focus on evidence criteria and context.

Transition Into Notes

Ask: “What evidence would we need to make a defensible energy decision rather than a slogan?”
