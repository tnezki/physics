The Coin That Refuses to Follow

A coin rests on a card over a cup. The card is about to move sideways very quickly.

Predict the coin’s path before we try it.

Commit to a prediction

When the card is snapped sideways, what will the coin do?

Follow the card?

Move sideways with it?

Do something else?

Describe the path you expect.

Be ready to name the interaction that would cause any sideways change in the coin’s motion.
Run the demo

Watch the coin, not the card.

What changed the card’s motion? What changed the coin’s motion?

Describe what you observed before explaining why it happened.

The puzzle

Does motion itself require a force?

Card

A hand gives it a large sideways change in motion.

Coin

What real interaction gives it a large sideways force?

Do not invent an “inertia force.” Ask what actually interacts with the object.
Question to carry into the notes

What should happen when the net force is zero?

Newton’s First Law is going to change the question from “What keeps it moving?” to “What causes its motion to change?”
