Physics - Unit 4 - Section 2 - Demo Teacher Guide

What Stayed the Same?

Inelastic cart-collision hook - designed for 5-8 minutes

Time5-8 minutes
FormatCart collision
Core ideaSystem momentum can be redistributed during a collision

Why This Demo Fits Section 4.2

Section 4.2 moves from momentum of one object to conservation of total momentum for a chosen system. A simple sticking collision creates the need for a system-level model without requiring numerical analysis yet.

Materials

  • 2 dynamics carts with hook-and-loop/Velcro bumpers
  • Level track or smooth tabletop

Before Class

  1. Level the track so stationary carts do not drift.
  2. Choose a moderate incoming speed that produces a clear sticking collision.

Suggested Flow

MinutesProjected PhaseTeacher Focus
0-2PredictionAsk what the two carts will do after they stick.
2-4Observe collisionRun once; repeat only if the class needs a clearer observation.
4-6One-object vs system viewContrast “the moving cart lost momentum” with “where did that momentum go?”
6-8BridgeIntroduce the idea of choosing a system, but save the conservation rule for notes.

Expected Observations / Student Thinking

The incoming cart slows and the pair moves together. Students often focus only on the incoming cart and describe its momentum as disappearing.

Watch for: Do not claim kinetic energy is conserved in the sticking collision. Section 4.2 is about momentum conservation; energy conservation is developed later.

Transition Into Notes

Ask: “What changes when we stop analyzing one cart and instead choose both carts as the system?”
