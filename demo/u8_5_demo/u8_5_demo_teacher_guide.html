Two Circuits. No Wire Between Them.

Low-voltage transformer reveal - designed for 5-8 minutes

Time5-8 minutes
FormatTwo-coil induction
Core ideaChanging magnetic field transfers energy

Why This Demo Fits Section 8.5

Section 8.5 introduces transformers and system-level analysis. Two electrically isolated coils on a shared core create a strong puzzle: voltage can appear in the secondary through a changing magnetic field, but energy conservation still controls the system.

Materials

  • Low-voltage AC source only
  • Iron transformer core
  • Primary coil
  • Secondary coil with visibly different turn count
  • AC voltmeter or small suitable indicator/load

Before Class

  1. Use a purpose-built low-voltage transformer kit if available.
  2. Measure primary and secondary voltages before class.
  3. Never use exposed mains voltage for the classroom demonstration.

Expected Observations / Evidence

The secondary responds to AC in the primary because the changing primary current creates a changing magnetic field that links both coils. More secondary turns than primary turns produces a larger secondary voltage in the idealized model.

Watch for: Use only low-voltage isolated AC. Do not expose students to mains circuits. Coils may warm if overloaded.

Transition Into Notes

The notes quantify the turns ratio and ideal power relationship, then apply the same field/force/induction/energy reasoning to motors, speakers, microphones, MRI, maglev, and extraordinary claims.
