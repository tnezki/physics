Unit 2 - Section 4 - Free Fall Motion

Which One Hits First?

Ignoring air resistance, free-falling objects share the same downward acceleration.

Watch, predict, and be ready to defend what you notice.

Make the prediction

Two compact objects have noticeably different masses. They are released from the same height at the same time.

Which hits first: heavier, lighter, or nearly together?
Commit to a reason

Do not just vote—choose the reason that supports your prediction.

Does mass change the free-fall acceleration, or is another effect responsible for many everyday differences?
Observe

Watch the simultaneous release carefully.

What did you actually observe? Separate observation from explanation.
Now change the shape

Compare a flat sheet of paper with the same paper crumpled tightly.

Why might the result change even though the amount of paper did not?
Question to carry into the notes

What must be true for “all objects fall alike” to be a useful physics model?

Keep this question unresolved until the section model gives us better language and evidence.
