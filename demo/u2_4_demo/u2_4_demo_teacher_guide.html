Physics - Unit 2 - Section 4 - Demo Teacher Guide

Which One Hits First?

Different-mass simultaneous drop - designed for 5-8 minutes

Time5-8 minutes
FormatDifferent-mass simultaneous drop
Core ideaIgnoring air resistance, free-falling objects share the same downward acceleration.

Why This Demo Fits Section 2.4

This hook happens before the notes and creates a need for the section idea without teaching the whole section. It stays within what students can notice or predict before formal instruction.

Materials

  • Two compact objects of clearly different mass; one sheet of paper; safe drop area.

Before Class

  1. Choose compact objects that can be dropped safely. Clear the floor and practice simultaneous release.

Suggested Flow

MinutesProjected PhaseTeacher Focus
0-1Opening phenomenonSet up the situation and require a prediction before explanation.
1-4Prediction / observationRun the live phenomenon or scenario. Ask for evidence, not vocabulary recitation.
4-6Compare claimsSurface competing interpretations and the misconception named below.
6-8Bridge questionStop before fully resolving the physics; carry the question into notes.

Expected Observations / Student Thinking

Ignoring air resistance, free-falling objects share the same downward acceleration. Students should be able to point to something they observed or inferred, even if they do not yet have precise vocabulary.

Watch for: Students may attribute everyday falling differences entirely to mass and ignore air resistance.

Reveal / Withhold

Reveal the physical result or graph evidence needed for the hook. Withhold a polished definition, formula derivation, or complete model until the guided notes.
