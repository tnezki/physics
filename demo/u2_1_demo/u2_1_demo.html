Unit 2 - Section 1 - Motion and Reference Frames

500 Miles, Almost Zero?

Motion depends on the reference frame used to describe it.

Watch, predict, and be ready to defend what you notice.

The claim

A race car travels 500 miles during a race.

At the finish line, is the car far from where it started?
Predict before we define anything

Choose the statement you think is best.

A: The car moved 500 miles. B: The car ended almost where it began. C: Both can be reasonable.
Change the observer

Imagine a camera bolted inside the car and a fan sitting in the grandstand.

Would those two observers describe the driver’s motion the same way?
Name what changed

The object did not change. The comparison point did.

What must a motion description include so two observers can understand each other?
Question to carry into the notes

How can the same object be moving in one description and at rest in another without either observer being wrong?

Keep this question unresolved until the section model gives us better language and evidence.
