Unit 3 - Section 3.1 - Force and Acceleration

Same Push, Different Result?

A cart is at rest. Which change should make its motion change more: a stronger push, or adding more mass?

Do not calculate yet. Commit to a prediction and a reason.

Round 1 - Change the push

Same cart. Two pushes.

Trial A

Give the cart a gentle, brief push.

Trial B

Give the same cart a clearly stronger, brief push.

What changed more: the cart's motion, or just the force you used?
Round 2 - Change the mass

Same kind of push. More mass.

Light cart

Push the empty cart.

Loaded cart

Add mass, then repeat as similarly as possible.

Which cart changes its motion more?
Competing models

Which idea fits both rounds?

Model A

More push always means more motion, no matter the mass.

Model B

The change in motion depends on both the push and how much mass must respond.

Question to carry into the notes

Can one rule connect force, mass, and acceleration?

What should happen to acceleration when force increases? What should happen when mass increases?
