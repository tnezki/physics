Physics - Unit 3 - Section 1 - Demo Teacher Guide

Same Push, Different Result?

Cart discrepant comparison - designed for 5-8 minutes

Time5-8 minutes
FormatCart discrepant comparison
Core ideaAcceleration depends on force and mass

Why This Demo Fits Section 3.1

The section begins by separating motion from acceleration and then introduces Newton's Second Law. This demo gives students two controlled comparisons - more push with the same mass, then more mass with a similar push - without front-loading the equation.

Materials

  • Low-friction dynamics cart
  • Track or smooth level surface
  • One or two cart masses

Before Class

  1. Level the track or choose a flat surface.
  2. Place one added mass where it cannot fall off.
  3. Keep the demonstration path clear.

Suggested Flow

MinutesProjected PhaseTeacher Focus
0-1Opening predictionGet a vote: stronger push, added mass, or both matter.
1-3Change the pushUse the same cart and visibly different brief pushes.
3-5Change the massAdd mass and repeat a similar push. Do not claim the pushes are quantitatively identical.
5-7Competing models / bridgeAsk for the pattern students think a law would need to explain.

Expected Observations / Student Thinking

Students should notice that a stronger push produces a larger change in motion, while added mass makes a similar push less effective at changing motion.

Watch for: Do not let students turn the demo into "force causes motion." Keep returning to change in velocity. Also avoid presenting the qualitative hand push as precision data.

Safety

Keep the cart on the track and use an end stop. Added masses must be secured so they cannot slide off.

Cleanup / Reset

Return masses and cart; no special cleanup is required.

Transition Into Notes

Use the final question to introduce the need for a quantitative relationship among net force, mass, and acceleration.
