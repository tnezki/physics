Physics - Unit 4 - Section 1 - Demo Teacher Guide

The Egg Has to Stop

Discrepant stopping-time demonstration - designed for 5-8 minutes

Time5-8 minutes
FormatTeacher demonstration
Core ideaSame momentum change can occur over different contact times

Why This Demo Fits Section 4.1

Section 4.1 introduces momentum, impulse, and the idea that increasing contact time can reduce average force for the same change in momentum. The egg landing makes the time difference visible before students learn the formal impulse relationships.

Materials

  • 2 identical raw eggs plus a rigid tray and a deep folded towel/foam landing area; or a reusable fragile-object substitute if raw eggs are impractical
  • Drop cloth or tray for cleanup
  • Paper towels / disinfectant as appropriate

Before Class

  1. Set the cushioned landing so the egg can come to rest over a visibly longer distance/time without bouncing out.
  2. Place the rigid tray inside a spill-control area.
  3. If using raw eggs, rehearse the drop height and keep students outside the landing zone.

Suggested Flow

MinutesProjected PhaseTeacher Focus
0-1Opening predictionGet a public commitment before introducing momentum or impulse vocabulary.
1-3Compare rigid vs cushioned stoppingEmphasize same object and similar incoming motion; direct attention to stopping time.
3-5Compare explanationsDo not yet formalize J=Ft; let students argue about what changed.
5-7Bridge questionName contact time only if needed and carry the unresolved force question into notes.

Expected Observations / Student Thinking

Students should notice that both eggs must change from moving to stopped, while the cushioned landing stretches that stopping interaction over more time and distance. Many will initially say the padding “absorbs the force” or “removes momentum.”

Watch for: Do not imply that padding eliminates the momentum change. The useful contrast is how the same overall stopping change can be spread over a longer time.

Transition Into Notes

Ask: “If the egg has to lose its incoming momentum either way, how can changing the stopping time change the force it experiences?”
