Unit 2 - Section 3 - Acceleration

Same Speed? Same Motion?

Acceleration is a change in velocity, not simply “going fast.”

Watch, predict, and be ready to defend what you notice.

Watch two walks

Your teacher will make two motion-detector walks.

Walk A: as steady as possible. Walk B: clearly change the motion.
Predict the graph

Before the second walk, predict what should change on the velocity-time graph.

Will velocity stay about the same, rise, fall, cross zero, or do something else?
Compare evidence

Look at the two velocity-time traces.

Which graph shows changing velocity? What evidence do you see?
The trap

A car can accelerate without getting faster.

What else besides speed can change velocity?
Question to carry into the notes

What single idea can describe speeding up, slowing down, and turning?

Keep this question unresolved until the section model gives us better language and evidence.
