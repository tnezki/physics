Physics - Unit 2 - Section 3 - Demo Teacher Guide

Same Speed? Same Motion?

Live motion-detector contrast - designed for 5-8 minutes

Time5-8 minutes
FormatLive motion-detector contrast
Core ideaAcceleration is a change in velocity, not simply “going fast.”

Why This Demo Fits Section 2.3

This hook happens before the notes and creates a need for the section idea without teaching the whole section. It stays within what students can notice or predict before formal instruction.

Materials

  • Vernier motion detector and Graphical Analysis; clear 3-4 m walking lane.

Before Class

  1. Connect the motion detector and display velocity-time data. Test the walking lane before class.

Suggested Flow

MinutesProjected PhaseTeacher Focus
0-1Opening phenomenonSet up the situation and require a prediction before explanation.
1-4Prediction / observationRun the live phenomenon or scenario. Ask for evidence, not vocabulary recitation.
4-6Compare claimsSurface competing interpretations and the misconception named below.
6-8Bridge questionStop before fully resolving the physics; carry the question into notes.

Expected Observations / Student Thinking

Acceleration is a change in velocity, not simply “going fast.” Students should be able to point to something they observed or inferred, even if they do not yet have precise vocabulary.

Watch for: Students may define acceleration as “moving fast” or “speeding up” only; hold the direction-change case for the closing prompt.

Reveal / Withhold

Reveal the physical result or graph evidence needed for the hook. Withhold a polished definition, formula derivation, or complete model until the guided notes.

Transition Into Notes

What single idea can describe speeding up, slowing down, and turning?
