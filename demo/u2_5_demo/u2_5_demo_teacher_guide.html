Physics - Unit 2 - Section 5 - Demo Teacher Guide

Match This Motion

Live motion-detector graph-matching challenge - designed for 5-8 minutes

Time5-8 minutes
FormatLive motion-detector graph-matching challenge
Core ideaA strong motion model tells the same story across graphs, vectors, diagrams, data, and words.

Why This Demo Fits Section 2.5

This hook happens before the notes and creates a need for the section idea without teaching the whole section. It stays within what students can notice or predict before formal instruction.

Materials

  • Vernier motion detector and Graphical Analysis; clear walking lane; target graph displayed in Graphical Analysis.

Before Class

  1. Connect the detector and prepare a reachable target graph; do not make the target so complex that the demo becomes a lab.

Suggested Flow

MinutesProjected PhaseTeacher Focus
0-1Opening phenomenonSet up the situation and require a prediction before explanation.
1-4Prediction / observationRun the live phenomenon or scenario. Ask for evidence, not vocabulary recitation.
4-6Compare claimsSurface competing interpretations and the misconception named below.
6-8Bridge questionStop before fully resolving the physics; carry the question into notes.

Expected Observations / Student Thinking

A strong motion model tells the same story across graphs, vectors, diagrams, data, and words. Students should be able to point to something they observed or inferred, even if they do not yet have precise vocabulary.

Watch for: Students may match one representation superficially while ignoring direction or timing consistency.

Reveal / Withhold

Reveal the physical result or graph evidence needed for the hook. Withhold a polished definition, formula derivation, or complete model until the guided notes.

Transition Into Notes

How can we test whether several different representations really describe the same motion?
