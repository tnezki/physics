Circle the better term in each pair.

  1. Wave returns from a gym wall: reflection / interference
  2. Two wave displacements add at the same point: interference / refraction
  3. A moving race car changes the frequency heard by a spectator: Doppler effect / beats

  5. A guitar body responds strongly near a natural frequency: resonance / reflection
