Activity 7A • Synthetic vs. NaturalJournal page 61 of 84
Name: ______________________________Date: _______________

As you read the article, Cars That Run on Air and Water? Audi Rolls Out E-Diesel consider the following questions. At the conclusion of the reading, find a partner or group and write your response:

1. Would you classify “e-diesel” as a synthetic product or a natural product? Why? Cite information from articles or research that supports your response.

2. What natural resources are used to make this product?

3. What chemical processes are used to make this product?

4. What are the negative and positive impacts to society of making and using the e-diesel fuel compared to making and using a more natural product with a similar function like regular diesel gasoline?
