Activity 6B • Investigating Burning: Products of CombustionJournal page 51 of 84
Name: ______________________________Date: _______________

1. On the Chemical Reaction Modeling Placemat, place the atoms together to make the molecules of the reactants (C₂₅H₅₂ and 38 O₂ molecules) on the left side of the chemical equation for the burning of the candle.

2. Write the chemical formula under each molecule of the reactants. Also draw a + sign between the reactants.

3. Rearrange the atoms in the reactants to make the molecules in the products on the right side of the arrow.

4. How many total atoms of each element are in the reactants?

5. How many total atoms of each element are in the products?

6. Write the chemical formula under each molecule of the products. Also draw a + sign between the products.

7. Compare the physical properties of the reactants and products.

8. Join another team and use the second set of Hydrocarbon Cards to make the reactants again to represent the chemical reaction as a complete chemical equation.

9. Write what you discovered about the number of atoms that make up the molecules on the reactant side and the number of atoms that make up the molecules on the product side of the equation.

10. Answer the class question: Where do the atoms of the products CO₂ and H₂O come from?
