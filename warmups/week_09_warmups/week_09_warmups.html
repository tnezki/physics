1Free Response2.3 · Acceleration and direction

A delivery robot moves west at 9 m/s. Three seconds later it is still moving west, but only at 3 m/s. Take east as positive. Find its average acceleration (include sign and units). Is the robot speeding up or slowing down? Explain.

2Free Response1.4 · Motion with balanced forces

A hockey puck glides straight across nearly frictionless ice at constant velocity after being struck. A classmate says, “Something must keep pushing it or it would stop.” Explain whether the puck needs a forward force to keep moving.

3Multiple Choice2.3 · Positive acceleration

A cart is moving to the right. Its velocity changes from +2 m/s to +8 m/s over 3 s. Which statement best describes its motion?

  1. It moves right and speeds up with positive acceleration.
  2. It moves left and speeds up with positive acceleration.
  3. It moves right and slows down with negative acceleration.
  4. It is not accelerating because it moves in one direction.
  5. It moves left and slows down with zero acceleration.

A cart moves in a straight line at constant velocity while a 14 N force pulls right and friction acts left. What must be true about the friction force?

  2. Friction is 28 N left because it is moving.
  3. Friction is 14 N left, giving zero net horizontal force.
  4. Friction is 14 N right, helping the pull.
  5. The friction cannot be compared without knowing the cart’s mass.
