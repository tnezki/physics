Check Your Understanding — Section 7.3: Resistance and Ohm’s Law

1. Define resistance and state its SI unit.

2. State how wire length and thickness generally affect resistance.

3. A 9 Ω resistor is connected across 27 V. Calculate the current.

4. A device carries 2 A at 14 V. Calculate resistance and explain what the $V/I$ ratio means.

5. A $V$–$I$ graph curves upward instead of forming a straight line through the origin. Explain what that suggests about resistance as current increases.

6. Design a test of whether a device behaves ohmically over a safe range. Specify the quantities you would vary and measure, the graph or ratios you would analyze, and the evidence that would support constant resistance.
