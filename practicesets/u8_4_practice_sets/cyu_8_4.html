Check Your Understanding — Section 8.4: Generators and Alternating Current

1. Define electromagnetic induction in terms of changing magnetic conditions.

2. State the main energy transformation in a generator and the defining direction behavior of alternating current.

3. A generator produces 15 cycles in 5 seconds. Calculate the frequency and identify what that frequency describes.

4. Compare moving the same magnet quickly and slowly through the same coil. Explain the induced-voltage difference.

5. Critique the statement: “A generator gets its electrical energy from the magnet itself.” Trace the energy source and the role of induction.

6. Design an investigation that demonstrates induction and tests the effect of changing motion rate. Specify the controlled variables, independent variable, measurement, and evidence that would support the relationship.
