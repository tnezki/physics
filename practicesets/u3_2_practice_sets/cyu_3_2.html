Check Your Understanding — Section 3.2: Friction and Real-World Forces

1. Name the friction type that can act on a box that has not begun to slide.

2. Distinguish mass from weight using meaning and units.

3. A 50-N push right is opposed by 20 N friction. Find net force.

4. A 7-kg object near Earth weighs about how many newtons? Then state what happens to its mass on the Moon.

5. Explain how a box can remain at rest while someone pushes it harder, as long as the push stays below the static-friction limit.

6. Propose evidence you would collect to compare two surfaces and decide which produces greater friction for the same box and applied-force setup.
