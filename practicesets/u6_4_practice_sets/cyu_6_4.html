Check Your Understanding — Section 6.4: Lenses and Optical Systems

1. Distinguish a converging lens from a diverging lens using parallel rays.

2. Define focal point, focal length, and real image.

3. A lens has $f=20$ cm and $d_o=60$ cm. Calculate $d_i$.

4. An object is 4 cm tall and its image is 6 cm tall. Calculate magnification magnitude and interpret the ratio.

5. Correct the claim “if an image is enlarged, it must be real.” Compare a magnifier with a projector.

6. Design a simple classroom projector model using a converging lens and a screen. State where the object must be relative to the focal point, what kind of image is required, what evidence would prove it is real, and one placement error that would prevent focus.
