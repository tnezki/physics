Check Your Understanding — Section 3.3: Free Fall and Nonfree Fall

Six-question DOK check for Section 3.3: 2 DOK 1, 2 DOK 2, 1 DOK 3, and 1 DOK 4.

1.DOK 1 · U3-S3-CYU1.01

Classify a rock falling in a vacuum as free fall or nonfree fall.

2.DOK 1 · U3-S3-CYU1.02

Identify which force model best represents terminal speed.

3.DOK 2 · U3-S3-CYU2.01

A falling object has 40 N weight and 40 N air resistance. Determine net force and acceleration.

4.DOK 2 · U3-S3-CYU2.02

5.DOK 3 · U3-S3-CYU3.01

6.DOK 4 · U3-S3-CYU4.01
