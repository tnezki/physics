Check Your Understanding — Section 8.2: Electricity and Magnetism

1. Describe the magnetic-field pattern around a straight current-carrying wire.

2. Explain how a compass can provide evidence for a magnetic field produced by current.

3. Compare a straight wire, one loop, and a many-turn coil in terms of how conductor shape changes the field pattern.

4. Predict what a fixed compass should do when current in a nearby wire is reversed, and explain why.

5. Critique the claim that the magnetic effect of a circuit is confined to the metal wire itself.

6. Design a comparison that would show both that current creates a magnetic field and that reversing current reverses field direction. Identify what must be held constant and what evidence you would collect.
