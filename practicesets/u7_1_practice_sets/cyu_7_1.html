Check Your Understanding — Section 7.1: Electric Charge and Forces

1. State the interaction between like charges and between opposite charges.

2. Distinguish a conductor from an insulator in terms of charge mobility.

3. A force is 36 N at separation $r$. Calculate the force at $2r$ with charge amounts unchanged.

4. A neutral metal sphere is near a positive rod. Explain the charge redistribution that occurs before contact.

5. Correct the claim “a rubbed object is charged because friction made extra electrons.” Use electron transfer and conservation of charge.

6. Design a short investigation to test the inverse-square distance prediction for electric force. Identify what you would hold constant, what you would vary, what evidence you would collect, and how the results would support or challenge the model.
