Check Your Understanding — Section 5.1: Vibrations and Wave Properties

1. Define wavelength and frequency in your own words.

2. Classify a sound wave in air as transverse or longitudinal and name one feature that supports the classification.

3. A wave has frequency 7 Hz and wavelength 1.2 m. Calculate its speed.

4. A wave travels at constant speed. Explain what happens to wavelength if frequency is increased.

5. Correct this statement: “A wave carries the medium from the source to the receiver.” Use particle motion and energy transfer in your revision.

6. Design a short observation or measurement that could test $v=f\lambda$ for waves on one rope. Identify what you would measure, what you would keep controlled, and what pattern in the data would support the relationship.
