Extra Practice — Section 6.2: Reflection and Mirrors

3. Define angle of reflection.

4. What does the normal line tell you about the geometry at the point where a ray strikes?

7. An incident ray makes 24° with the normal. Find the reflected angle and the angle between the incident and reflected rays.

8. A child walks 0.40 m closer to a plane mirror. Describe how the object–image separation changes.
