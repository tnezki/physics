Practice Set 1 — Section 6.2: Reflection and Mirrors

1. Define reflection in terms of what happens to a light ray at a surface.

2. Name the perpendicular reference line used to measure reflection angles.

3. Distinguish the incident ray from the reflected ray.

4. State the law of reflection in symbols or words.

5. What surface property tends to produce a clear reflected image?

6. Use the labeled reflection diagram as a reference. Predict how the reflected ray would rotate if the incident ray were moved closer to the normal while the mirror stayed fixed, and justify your prediction with the law of reflection.

7. A ray reaches a plane mirror at 28° from the normal. Determine the angle of reflection.

8. A student stands 1.5 m in front of a plane mirror. Where does the image appear relative to the mirror, and what is the eye actually receiving?

9. A beam strikes a mirror along the normal line. Predict the reflected path and explain using the law of reflection.

10. Two surfaces receive the same beam: polished metal and matte paper. Compare the reflected-ray directions and the kind of image each can produce.

11. A student says, “A mirror reverses left and right.” Explain a more accurate front–back interpretation using the image position.

12. A ray diagram measures the incidence angle from the mirror surface. Diagnose the error and describe how the diagram and angle value should be corrected.
