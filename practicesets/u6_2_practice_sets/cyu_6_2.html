Check Your Understanding — Section 6.2: Reflection and Mirrors

1. Label the roles of incident ray, reflected ray, and normal line in a reflection model.

2. Explain the difference between specular and diffuse reflection.

3. A ray strikes a mirror at 52° from the normal. Determine the reflected angle and state the law used.

4. An object is 75 cm from a plane mirror. Describe the image location and why the image is virtual.

5. Correct the claim “a mirror reverses left and right” using ray behavior and front–back reversal.

6. Design a short investigation to test the law of reflection. State what you would measure, what pattern would support the law, and one likely measurement error.
