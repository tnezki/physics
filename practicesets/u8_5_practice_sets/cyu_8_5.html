Check Your Understanding — Section 8.5: Modern Electromagnetic Systems

1. State the function of a transformer and distinguish primary from secondary coil.

2. State the main energy transformations for a motor and a generator.

3. A transformer has 300 primary turns, 900 secondary turns, and 18 V on the primary. Determine the secondary voltage and classify the transformer.

4. An ideal transformer delivers 200 W at 50 V on its secondary. Determine the secondary current and explain why a higher voltage does not imply free energy.

5. Compare a speaker and microphone as reverse-direction electromagnetic systems, tracing input, magnetic interaction, and output.

6. Evaluate a proposed “magnet-powered” device that claims continuous 500 W output with no input. Specify the conservation issue, the measurements required, and what evidence would be needed before accepting the claim.
