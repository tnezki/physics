Check Your Understanding — Section 5.2: Wave Interactions

1. Define constructive and destructive interference.

2. State the difference between a node and an antinode.

3. Two overlapping displacements are +5 cm and −3 cm. Find the resultant and classify the overlap as reinforcing or partially canceling.

4. Explain why an approaching source is observed at a higher frequency without claiming that the wave speed through the medium increases.

5. Critique the claim “a standing wave is just one wave frozen in place.” Use reflection, superposition, nodes, and antinodes.

6. Create and defend a model for a string that forms a stable standing wave. Describe the incoming and reflected waves, what data or observations would identify nodes/antinodes, and one misconception your model must avoid.
