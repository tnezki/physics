Check Your Understanding — Section 1.3: Net Force and Vectors

1. Explain why force is a vector and give one complete force description.

2. A box has 32 N right and 14 N left. Determine the net force and direction.

3. A sled has 9 N right, 13 N right, and 8 N left. Determine the net force.

4. Explain what arrow length and arrowhead direction communicate in a scaled force diagram.

5. Critique the claim “Equal force magnitudes always cancel.” Give one cancelling example and one non-cancelling example.

6. Two perpendicular force vectors act on one object. Explain how a resultant vector can represent their combined effect and what a physically reasonable resultant direction should look like.
