Check Your Understanding — Section 3.1: Force and Acceleration

1. A cart moves east at constant velocity. State its acceleration and horizontal net force.

2. A 14 N force acts right while 6 N acts left. State net force and acceleration direction.

3. A 5-kg cart experiences 30 N net force. Calculate acceleration.

4. Two carts feel 18 N net force; their masses are 3 kg and 9 kg. Calculate and compare accelerations.

5. Explain why a moving object can have a net force opposite its velocity and what that does to speed initially.

6. Design a simple two-trial test that would show acceleration is directly proportional to net force while mass is held constant. State the variables and expected evidence.
