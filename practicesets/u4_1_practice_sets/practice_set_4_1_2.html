Practice Set 2 — Section 4.1: Momentum and Impulse

1. Define momentum without using the phrase “amount of motion.” Include both quantities that determine it.

2. If velocity is chosen as negative, what sign does momentum have for a positive mass?

3. What is impulse: something an object possesses while moving, or something it experiences during an interaction?

4. State the impulse–momentum relationship.

5. Name one safety feature whose purpose is to lengthen stopping time.

6. A 0.40 kg ball moves west at 15 m/s. Calculate its momentum, using west as negative.

7. A 45 N force acts for 0.16 s. Find the impulse delivered.

8. Use the figure to compare the two pushes. If the force is the same in both cases but one lasts twice as long, explain how the impulses compare.

9. A cart’s momentum changes from −6 kg·m/s to +2 kg·m/s. Calculate the impulse, including sign.

10. A stopping interaction requires 20 N·s of impulse. Find the average force if the stop lasts 0.10 s, then predict the force if the time is doubled.

11. A student claims, “If two objects have equal speed, the heavier one always has exactly twice the momentum.” Identify what information is missing and repair the statement.

12. A catcher moves the glove backward while catching a fast ball. Explain the effect on stopping time, average force, and change in momentum.

13. Momentum is zero when

  1. velocity is zero
  2. mass is large
  3. force is zero
  4. contact time is long
  5. impulse is large

14. Which pair could have equal momentum?

  1. 2 kg at 6 m/s and 3 kg at 4 m/s
  2. 2 kg at 6 m/s and 3 kg at 6 m/s
  3. 1 kg at 2 m/s and 4 kg at 2 m/s
  4. 5 kg at 1 m/s and 5 kg at 5 m/s
  5. 8 kg at rest and 1 kg at 8 m/s

15. Momentum points in the direction of the object’s

  1. velocity
  2. mass
  3. contact time
  4. impulse duration only
  5. kinetic energy

16. Which situation produces the greatest impulse if force magnitude is the same?

  1. The force acts for the longest time.
  2. The force acts for the shortest time.
  3. The object has the greatest mass.
  4. The object begins at rest.
  5. The force is balanced by another force.

17. $J=\Delta p$ means that impulse equals the

  1. change in momentum
  2. initial momentum
  3. final momentum only
  4. mass times time
  5. force divided by time

18. A 10 N force maintained for 0.50 s gives an impulse of

  1. 0.05 N·s
  2. 5 N·s
  3. 10 N·s
  4. 20 N·s
  5. 50 N·s

19. A crumple zone reduces injury mainly by

  1. increasing stopping time for a similar momentum change
  2. eliminating the passenger’s initial momentum before impact
  3. making impulse zero
  4. increasing average force
  5. making mass disappear

20. Which statement is most accurate for two catches of the same ball from the same incoming speed?

  1. A longer stopping time can give a smaller average force for the same momentum change.
  2. A longer stop must increase average force.
  3. The shorter stop always gives less impulse.
  4. Momentum change is zero if the glove moves.
  5. Contact time has no relation to force.
