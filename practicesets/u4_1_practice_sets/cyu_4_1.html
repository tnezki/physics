Check Your Understanding — Section 4.1: Momentum and Impulse

1. A 4 kg cart moves at 3 m/s. Calculate its momentum.

2. State the relationship between impulse and change in momentum.

3. A 50 N force acts for 0.08 s. Determine the impulse.

4. The same 16 N·s momentum change occurs in 0.04 s and 0.20 s. Calculate both average forces and compare.

5. Explain why a heavier object does not automatically have more momentum than a lighter object.

6. Design a short test comparing two stopping materials for the same moving cart. State what should be held constant, what should be measured, and what evidence would support the contact-time/force model.
