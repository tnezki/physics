Check Your Understanding — Section 1.2: Forces and Newton’s First Law

1. State Newton’s First Law in your own words and identify the condition that allows motion to remain unchanged.

2. Explain why inertia is a property of matter rather than a force. Give one example.

3. A book rests on a table. Identify its two main vertical forces and explain why this is “no net force,” not “no forces.”

4. A puck slows after entering a rough patch. Use Newton’s First Law to explain what this tells you about the net force.

5. Critique the claim “A moving object must have a forward force larger than all backward forces.” Give a corrected statement that includes constant-speed motion.

6. Two students explain a tablecloth trick. One treats inertia as a force; the other treats inertia as resistance to a change in motion. Defend the stronger explanation using at least two physics terms.
