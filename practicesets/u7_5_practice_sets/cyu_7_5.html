Check Your Understanding — Section 7.5: Complex Electric Circuits

1. Distinguish series and parallel circuits by the number and arrangement of current paths.

2. Explain why a complete conducting path is required for steady current.

3. Calculate the equivalent resistance of 3 Ω, 4 Ω, and 5 Ω resistors in series.

4. Calculate the equivalent resistance of two 6 Ω resistors in parallel and check that the result is below either branch resistance.

5. Explain why one open bulb has different effects in a series string and in a parallel branch network.

6. Design a safe three-load household-style circuit. Include independent branches, branch switches, a common protective device, and a brief explanation of what happens during an overload and when one branch is opened.
