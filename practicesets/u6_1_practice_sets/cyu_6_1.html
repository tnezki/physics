Check Your Understanding — Section 6.1: Nature of Light

1. Explain why light is classified as an electromagnetic wave rather than a mechanical wave.

2. Distinguish transparent, translucent, and opaque materials using what happens to transmitted light.

3. Calculate the frequency of a $7.5\times10^{-7}$ m light wave in vacuum.

4. Explain why a larger extended light source can create a penumbra instead of a perfectly sharp shadow edge.

5. Correct the claim “we see objects because our eyes send light toward them.” Use a source–object–eye path.

6. Design a simple test to compare two materials for a museum window. State what evidence you would collect for transmission, scattering, and shadow formation, and how the evidence would support your classification.
