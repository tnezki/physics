Check Your Understanding — Section 7.2: Electric Potential and Current

1. Define voltage and current in words and include the quantity each compares.

2. Explain why a complete path matters for sustained current.

3. A source transfers 84 J to 7 C. Calculate the voltage.

4. A current carries 30 C past a point in 12 s. Calculate the current.

5. Correct the statement “voltage flows out of the battery and is used up by the bulb.” Use potential difference, current, and energy transfer.

6. Create an evidence-based model for a simple battery–lamp circuit that distinguishes where charge is, what flows, what the source maintains, and where energy is transferred. Include one measurement that could test your model.
