Check Your Understanding — Section 5.3: Nature of Sound

1. Explain why a vibrating source is necessary for sound.

2. Define compression and rarefaction.

3. A sound travels 850 m in 2.5 s. Calculate its speed.

4. Compare pitch and loudness by linking each to the relevant wave property.

5. Critique the claim “sound moves because air particles fly from the speaker to your ear.” Replace it with a correct particle-and-energy model.

6. A student claims sound cannot travel through solids. Design an evidence-based classroom test using available solid and air paths, identify what would count as evidence, and explain how the result would address the misconception.
