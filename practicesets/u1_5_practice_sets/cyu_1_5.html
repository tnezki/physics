Check Your Understanding — Section 1.5: Modeling Forces in the Real World

1. Describe the steps you would use to build a force model for a backpack resting on the floor.

2. A cart has 35 N right and 35 N left. Give two motion descriptions that could fit the model.

3. A box moves right while a 25-N net force points left. Explain how its motion can be consistent with that force direction.

4. A hanging object is supported by two angled ropes at rest. Describe the three main force vectors and the net-force condition.

5. Critique a force diagram that includes a forward “motion force” only because the object is moving forward. Revise the model in words.

6. A scenario, force diagram, and motion description do not agree. Explain a systematic process for identifying the mismatch and revising one representation using physics vocabulary.
