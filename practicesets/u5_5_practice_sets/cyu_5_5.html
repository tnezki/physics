Check Your Understanding — Section 5.5: Sound Interference and Beats

1. Define beats and destructive interference.

2. State what it means for two equal sound waves to be in phase versus out of phase.

3. Two tones are 432 Hz and 438 Hz. Calculate the beat frequency.

4. Explain why beat rate decreases as two instrument frequencies are brought closer together.

5. Critique the statement “noise-canceling headphones erase sound energy.” Replace it with a superposition-based explanation.

6. A headphone designer tests cancellation at several frequencies and phase delays. Describe an investigation that would identify the best settings, what data to collect, and how the evidence would support or challenge the design claim.
