Check Your Understanding — Section 5.5: Sound Interference and Beats

Six-question DOK check for Section 5.5: 2 DOK 1, 2 DOK 2, 1 DOK 3, and 1 DOK 4.

1.DOK 1 · U5-S5-CYU1.01

Concept check during the noise-control and beats review: Give the expected change in causes beats: two close frequencies or one steady frequency. Answer as if you were checking another student’s model.

2.DOK 1 · U5-S5-CYU1.02

Concept check during the noise-control and beats review: If two tuning frequencies become closer together, state whether the beats become faster or slower. Answer as if you were checking another student’s model.

3.DOK 2 · U5-S5-CYU2.01

Independent practice — noise-control and beats review: A peer reviewer proposes canceling a 500 Hz tone with a 700 Hz tone of equal amplitude. Explain why frequency mismatch prevents stable strong cancellation. Interpret the result in the situation, not just as a number.

4.DOK 2 · U5-S5-CYU2.02

Independent practice — noise-control and beats review: Analyze the beat waveform graph to identify whether the rapid oscillation or the slow amplitude envelope corresponds to the beat pattern heard by a listener. Interpret the result in the situation, not just as a number.

5.DOK 3 · U5-S5-CYU3.01

Model critique — noise-control and beats review: A musician hears faster beats after adjusting a string and concludes the strings are closer in frequency. Critique the conclusion and correct it. Finish with a corrected physics statement.

6.DOK 4 · U5-S5-CYU4.01

Transfer challenge — noise-control and beats review: A musician tunes an instrument by listening to beats. Build an evidence-based tuning procedure that explains what the beat rate measures, what happens as frequencies approach one another, how the musician recognizes a near match, and one ambiguity beat frequency alone cannot resolve. Defend the model or design and identify what evidence would strengthen it.
