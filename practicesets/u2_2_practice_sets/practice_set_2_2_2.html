Practice Set 2 — Section 2.2: Speed and Velocity

1. Define average speed using the words total distance and total time.

2. Which term describes the reading of a car speedometer at one particular moment?

3. Write one complete velocity statement for a skateboarder moving at 4 m/s.

4. What is the difference between constant speed and constant velocity?

5. Classify 22 m/s east as scalar or vector, and identify the feature that decides.

6. A cart covers 225 m in 18 s. Determine its average speed with units.

7. A shuttle moves at a constant 12 m/s for 35 s. Determine the distance it travels.

8. Use the circular-track image. Explain how the car can keep the same speed while its velocity changes.

9. A cyclist averages 15.0 m/s over a 360-m route. Determine the travel time and show the relationship you used.

10. Two trips each cover 120 km in 2 h. One is nearly steady; the other includes stops and bursts of higher speed. Compare the average speeds and explain why their motion histories can still differ.

11. A student says, “The trip computer says 40 km/h, so the car must have traveled 40 km/h the whole time.” Evaluate the claim and describe one motion history consistent with a 40 km/h average.

12. Two cars both maintain 15 m/s. Car A travels straight north; Car B follows a curved road. Compare their speed and velocity behavior and explain what evidence distinguishes the cases.

  1. 3 m/s
  2. 5 m/s

  1. instantaneous speed
  2. average speed
  3. total distance
  4. velocity only if no direction is known
  5. elapsed time

  1. 12 m/s
  2. 12 m/s south
  3. 5 km/h east

  5. 3 m/s west

16. Speed is called a scalar because it has

  1. magnitude without required direction
  2. direction without magnitude
  3. no units
  4. two components

17. Which change guarantees a change in velocity?

  1. a change in direction
  2. a new paint color

18. Which situation best represents constant velocity?

  1. moving straight west at 6 m/s steadily
  2. circling at 6 m/s

19. If a traveler covers 240 km in 4 h, the average speed is

  1. 40 km/h
  2. 60 km/h
  3. 80 km/h

20. Which pair can have equal speeds but unequal velocities?

  1. two cars at 10 m/s in opposite directions
  2. two cars at rest side by side
  3. two trains both 12 m/s east
  4. two carts both 4 m/s north
  5. one object compared with itself
