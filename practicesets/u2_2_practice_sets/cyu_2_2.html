Check Your Understanding — Section 2.2: Speed and Velocity

1. Distinguish speed from velocity.

2. Distinguish average speed from instantaneous speed.

3. A cart travels 132 m in 11 s. Determine average speed and interpret it.

4. A runner maintains 6 m/s for 35 s. Determine distance traveled.

5. Explain why two cars can have equal speeds but different velocities. Give a specific example.

6. Design two different one-minute motion histories with the same average speed but clearly different instantaneous speeds. State distances/times that make the averages equal and explain the difference.
