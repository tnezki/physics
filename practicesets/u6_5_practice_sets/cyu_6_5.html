Check Your Understanding — Section 6.5: Optical Applications

1. Identify the main optical behavior used by a periscope, a camera lens, and an optical fiber.

2. Explain the purpose of a corrective lens without saying it “makes the eye stronger.”

3. A fiber has core $n=1.50$ and cladding $n=1.38$. Calculate the critical angle and determine whether a 70° internal ray satisfies the TIR condition.

4. Explain why one anti-glare observation at one angle is not enough evidence for a universal claim.

5. Correct the statement “a mirage is water on the road reflecting the sky.” Trace the actual light behavior conceptually.

6. A museum wants clear viewing, reduced glare, and reduced damaging radiation. Propose an optical display design, identify at least three light interactions or material properties that matter, and state measurements that would test whether the design meets the goals.
