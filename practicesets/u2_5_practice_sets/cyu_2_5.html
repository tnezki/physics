Check Your Understanding — Section 2.5: Velocity Vectors and Motion Modeling

1. State what vector length and direction each represent.

2. Describe the dot-spacing pattern for constant speed.

3. Two equal-length velocity arrows point east then north. Interpret the speed and velocity change.

4. A table increases by 4 m every second. Describe a matching graph and motion diagram.

5. A graph curves upward while a story says constant speed. Critique the mismatch and revise one representation.

6. Create or describe a complete motion model for an object that speeds up and then turns at constant speed. Your model must connect a data or graph representation, velocity vectors, and a written explanation, and you must defend how the representations agree.
