Check Your Understanding — Section 8.1: Magnetism and Magnetic Fields

1. State the interaction of two facing north poles and of a north pole facing a south pole.

2. Explain what field-line arrows and field-line spacing each represent on a magnetic-field model.

3. A compass is moved from a dense-line region to a sparse-line region on the same field map. Predict how field strength changes and what the compass still tells you at either location.

4. A piece of iron is magnetized and later partially demagnetized. Use domain alignment to explain both changes.

5. Critique the statement: “Cutting a magnet separates its north and south poles.” Use evidence from ordinary magnets to support your revision.

6. Design a short classroom investigation for mapping both direction and relative strength around a bar magnet. Specify the tool, what you would record, and how the observations would support the field model.
