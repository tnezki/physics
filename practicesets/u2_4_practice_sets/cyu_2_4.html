Check Your Understanding — Section 2.4: Free Fall Motion

1. Define free fall.

2. State the approximate value of $g$ used in this unit.

3. A ball drops from rest for 3 s. Determine final speed and total distance.

4. Use total distances 5, 20, 45 m to determine the separate distances traveled during the first three seconds.

5. Explain why a coin and feather can land at different times in air but together in vacuum.

6. A student sketches equal distances for each second of a dropped ball. Critique the model, describe a corrected distance pattern, and identify what evidence would distinguish the two models.
