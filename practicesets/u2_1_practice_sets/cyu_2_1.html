Check Your Understanding — Section 2.1: Motion and Reference Frames

1. Define reference frame and give one classroom example.

2. A lunchbox rests on a moving bus seat. Describe it relative to the seat and road.

3. Rewrite “the student moved” as two different precise statements using two different frames.

4. Explain why Earth’s surface is convenient for everyday motion but not the only valid frame.

5. Two observers disagree about whether a drone is moving. Explain what evidence about their frames is needed to resolve the disagreement.

6. Create a short scenario with one object and two observers in which both “moving” and “at rest” are correct. Defend each description by explicitly naming its reference frame.
