Check Your Understanding — Section 8.3: Electromagnets and Magnetic Forces

1. List two controllable changes that can make an electromagnet stronger.

2. State what $q$, $v$, $B$, $I$, and $L$ represent in the allowed magnetic-force formulas.

3. A $0.60\text{ C}$ charge moves at $3.0\text{ m/s}$ perpendicular to a $2.5\text{ T}$ field. Calculate the magnetic-force magnitude.

4. A $0.40\text{ m}$ wire carrying $5.0\text{ A}$ experiences $2.0\text{ N}$ of perpendicular magnetic force. Determine the field strength.

5. Explain why switching off the current in a lifting electromagnet can release a load, including the role of the core.

6. Design a fair test for comparing three electromagnet designs. Specify which variables you would change, which you would control, what strength data you would record, and how you would choose the best design.
