Check Your Understanding — Section 7.4: Power and Electrical Energy

1. Define electric power and distinguish watts from watt-hours.

2. State the relationships $P=IV$ and $E=Pt$ in words.

3. A 120 V device draws 1.5 A. Calculate power.

4. A 90 W device runs for 2.5 h. Calculate electrical energy use in Wh.

5. Correct the claim “the device with the largest watt rating always uses the most energy.” Include operating time in your explanation.

6. Design a small household energy audit for three devices. Specify what measurements or labels you would collect, how you would calculate daily energy, how you would compare devices fairly, and what additional evidence would be needed before calling one option more efficient.
