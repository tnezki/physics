Check Your Understanding — Section 2.3: Acceleration

1. State what acceleration measures.

2. Name two ways an object can accelerate without simply “going faster.”

3. Velocity changes from 4 m/s to 19 m/s in 5 s. Determine acceleration.

4. A car turns at constant speed. Explain why velocity changes.

5. Critique: “If speed is constant, acceleration is zero.” Use a circular-motion example.

6. Design a short cart investigation that could distinguish constant velocity from constant acceleration. Identify what you would measure and what data pattern would support each model.
