Practice Set 2 — Section 2.3: Acceleration

1. Write the acceleration relationship using change in velocity and elapsed time.

2. A car speeds up while moving in the positive direction. What sign of acceleration does this normally have?

3. A vehicle moves in a straight line with unchanged speed and direction. Classify its acceleration.

4. Can an object accelerate while its speed stays constant? Give the type of motion that makes this possible.

5. What does the symbol $\Delta v$ mean in the acceleration formula?

6. A runner changes from 2 m/s to 14 m/s in 6 s. Determine the average acceleration.

7. A bicycle moving east slows from 10 m/s to 4 m/s in 2 s. Take east as positive and calculate acceleration.

8. Use the circular-track figure. Explain why equal speed around the track does not mean zero acceleration.

9. A cart has velocities 0, 3, 6, 9, 12 m/s at successive one-second times. Calculate acceleration and explain what stays constant in the pattern.

10. A velocity–time graph slopes downward from +16 m/s at 0 s to 0 m/s at 4 s. Determine the acceleration and describe the motion.

11. A classmate claims, “If the velocity arrows are the same length, acceleration must be zero.” Evaluate the claim and explain what additional vector feature must be checked.

12. Two cars both read 20 m/s on their speedometers. Car A travels straight east; Car B follows a curved exit ramp. Compare their accelerations and defend the difference using velocity change.

13. Acceleration is the rate at which

  1. velocity changes
  2. distance exists
  3. time stops
  4. mass changes
  5. speed is measured only

14. If velocity changes from 6 to 18 m/s in 4 s, acceleration is

  1. 2 m/s²
  2. 3 m/s²
  3. 4.5 m/s²
  4. 6 m/s²
  5. 24 m/s²

15. Which is evidence of negative acceleration when motion is positive?

  1. speed decreases
  2. speed increases
  3. speed stays constant and direction stays fixed
  4. the object is at rest for all time
  5. distance is positive

16. Which situation can have constant speed and nonzero acceleration?

  1. a car rounding a curve
  2. a train moving straight steadily
  3. a parked bicycle
  4. a book on a desk
  5. a cart with constant velocity

17. A flat velocity–time graph indicates

  1. zero acceleration
  2. increasing acceleration
  3. decreasing time
  4. changing direction automatically
  5. free fall

18. A straight upward-sloping velocity–time graph indicates

  1. constant positive acceleration
  2. constant velocity
  3. zero velocity
  4. negative time
  5. no displacement

19. If a cart’s velocity changes by +4 m/s every second, its acceleration is

  1. +4 m/s²
  2. +1 m/s²
  3. 0 m/s²
  4. -4 m/s²
  5. +4 m/s

20. A driver turns north while keeping the same speed. What changed?

  1. velocity and therefore acceleration is nonzero
  2. speed only
  3. nothing
  4. mass only
  5. time only
