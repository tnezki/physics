Check Your Understanding — Section 1.4: Equilibrium and Balanced Motion

1. Define mechanical equilibrium and distinguish static from dynamic equilibrium.

2. A 160-N sign hangs at rest from one vertical cable. Determine the cable tension and state the net force.

3. A person stands still on a scale. Explain why a large scale reading can coexist with zero net force.

4. A cart moves straight at constant speed while a 52-N push acts right. Determine the required leftward resistance.

5. Critique the claim “If an object is moving, the forward force must be larger.” Use dynamic equilibrium in your response.

6. A platform is supported by two vertical ropes whose tensions are unequal. Explain how the platform can still be in equilibrium and what condition the two tensions must satisfy together with the weight.
