Check Your Understanding — Section 1.4: Equilibrium and Balanced Motion

Six-question DOK check for Section 1.4: 2 DOK 1, 2 DOK 2, 1 DOK 3, and 1 DOK 4.

1.DOK 1 · U1-S4-CYU1.01

Define mechanical equilibrium in terms of net force.

2.DOK 1 · U1-S4-CYU1.02

Name the downward gravitational force on an object near Earth.

3.DOK 2 · U1-S4-CYU2.01

A person stands still on a scale reading 300 N. State the support force, weight, and net force.

4.DOK 2 · U1-S4-CYU2.02

A hanging object has 120 N of weight downward. Work out the total upward force required for equilibrium.

5.DOK 3 · U1-S4-CYU3.01

A physics student says, “The scale reads 600 N, so the net force on me is 600 N upward.” Critique the statement and explain the role of weight.

6.DOK 4 · U1-S4-CYU4.01

Compare two explanations of a rolling bust moving straight at constant speed: one says the push exceeds friction because the rolling bust moves; the other says the horizontal forces balance. Defend the stronger model using equilibrium and Newton’s First Law.
