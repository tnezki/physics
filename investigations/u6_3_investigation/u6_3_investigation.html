Bending Light and Finding the TIR Boundary

Measure refraction in acrylic, then increase the internal angle until transmission gives way to total internal reflection.

Learning target: Explain refraction using changes in light speed, apply refractive-index relationships, and identify the conditions for total internal reflection.
Research Question: How do ray angles change when light enters a slower medium, and what changes when the internal incident angle becomes large enough for total internal reflection?
