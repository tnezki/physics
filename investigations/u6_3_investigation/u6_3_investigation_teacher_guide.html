Physics - Unit 6 - Section 6.3 - Investigation Teacher Guide

Bending Light and Finding the TIR Boundary

Quantitative refraction/TIR lab - designed for 25-30 minutes

Time25-30 minutes
Groups2-3
Core toolSemicircular acrylic block + laser

Why This Investigation Fits Section 6.3

The 6.3 notes authorize n = c/v, Snell’s law, and the critical-angle relationship. The semicircular block makes both bending and the onset of total internal reflection directly visible while reducing unwanted refraction at the curved face.

Materials Per Group

  • Semicircular acrylic block
  • Low-power laser or ray box
  • White paper
  • Ruler and protractor
  • Tape
  • Calculator

Before Class

  1. Check blocks for clean flat faces.
  2. Pre-test the beam geometry so the ray passes through the center of curvature.
  3. Mark a safe beam zone below eye level.

Suggested Flow

Expected Evidence

Air-to-acrylic rays should bend toward the normal and give an acrylic index near the accepted classroom value (often around 1.49, depending on material). Acrylic-to-air rays bend away from the normal until the critical angle is reached; beyond it the transmitted ray disappears and internal reflection dominates.

Sample Reasoning / Calculation

Example only: if a trial gives θair = 40° and θacrylic = 25°, n ≈ sin40°/sin25° ≈ 1.52. Then sinθc ≈ 1/1.52 ≈ 0.658, so θc ≈ 41°. Student values should be judged against their actual block and measurement uncertainty.

Watch for: Students often reverse toward/away rules or think total internal reflection can happen entering the higher-index medium from air.

Strong Responses

PromptWhat to Look For
Refraction directionConnects toward/away bending to slower/faster travel across the boundary.
Index estimateUses measured angles consistently from the normal.
TIR explanationStates higher-index to lower-index travel plus an incident angle greater than the critical angle.

Common Problems

  • Unexpected second bend: Ray is not passing through the center of the semicircle; realign geometry.
  • No clear TIR transition: Darken the room, clean the block, and scan angles more slowly.
  • Index values scatter: Check angle reference, center point, and protractor alignment.

Safety / Cleanup

Keep laser/ray-box beams below eye level. Do not look into the beam through the acrylic block.
