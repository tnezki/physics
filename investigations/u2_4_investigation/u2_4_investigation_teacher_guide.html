Physics - Unit 2 - Section 4 - Investigation Teacher Guide

Full-period ball-drop investigation with video analysis

Full-period ball-drop investigation with video analysis - designed for 40 minutes

Time40 minutes
Groups3-4
Core toolVernier Graphical Analysis video analysis

Why This Investigation Fits Section 2.4

Describe free fall as motion under gravity alone and use simple free-fall relationships to determine speed or distance fallen. The activity comes after the notes, so students use the section model to interpret evidence rather than discovering later-unit content prematurely.

Materials Per Group

  • 2-3 compact balls of different masses, one sheet of paper per group, meter stick, phone/tablet camera or Vernier video capture, Vernier Graphical Analysis, safe drop area

Before Class

  1. Set a safe drop zone and a visible scale for video analysis. Use compact, non-breakable objects.

Suggested Flow

Expected Evidence

Compact objects should be consistent with a downward acceleration near 10 m/s² within video/scale uncertainty. Mass alone should not create a clear difference in free-fall acceleration; flat paper should show more air-resistance effect than crumpled paper.

Watch for: Do not let students climb on furniture. Teacher-controlled elevated release is preferable if more height is needed.

Strong Responses

FeatureWhat to Look For
Claim / conclusionDirectly answers the research question rather than restating procedure.
EvidenceUses specific measured values, graph trends, or observed relationships.
ReasoningUses only the Section 2.4 physics model and connects evidence to the claim.

Common Problems

  • Messy data: Repeat one clean trial instead of averaging several poor runs.
  • Direction confusion: Have students state the chosen positive direction or reference frame before interpreting signs or motion.
  • Overreach: If students introduce later concepts, redirect them to the representations and formulas currently established in Unit 2.

Safety / Cleanup

Keep walking and drop zones clear, use non-breakable equipment, and reset sensors/carts/materials for the next class. For the free-fall investigation, do not allow students to climb on desks or chairs.
