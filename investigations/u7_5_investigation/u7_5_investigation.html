Physics - Unit 7 - Section 7.5 - Investigation

One Path or Many?

Use real circuits and resistance calculations to compare series and parallel behavior.

Learning target: Trace current paths, compare voltage/current in series and parallel circuits, calculate equivalent resistance, and explain circuit protection.
Research Question: What evidence distinguishes one-path and branched circuits, and why does that difference matter for resistance and safety?

1. Predict the Two Circuits

For two identical bulbs, predict brightness and what happens to the second bulb if the first is removed in (A) a one-path circuit and (B) a two-branch circuit.

4. Equivalent Resistance Test

Use labeled 100-Ω and 220-Ω resistors. Calculate the equivalent resistance first for series, then for parallel.

Check your answers physically: the series result should be larger than either resistor; the parallel result should be smaller than either resistor.

5. Safety Scenario

A household branch already supplies several devices. Another high-power device is switched on, total current rises, and the breaker opens. Explain how adding parallel loads can increase total current and why opening the circuit protects the wiring. Include the idea of overload, not “extra current needing somewhere to go.”

6. Circuit Design Claim

You are designing classroom lights. One failed lamp should not turn all others off. Choose series, parallel, or mixed architecture and defend the choice using current paths and voltage.
