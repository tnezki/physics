Physics - Unit 7 - Section 7.3 - Investigation

Does Every Device Obey Ohm's Law the Same Way?

Build voltage-current evidence for a fixed resistor and a glowing filament.

Learning target: Use Ohm’s Law, predict how voltage/resistance affect current, and interpret voltage-current graphs.
Research Question: How can voltage-current data reveal whether a device has approximately constant resistance?

1. Predict Two V-I Patterns

You will test a fixed resistor and a miniature incandescent bulb. Predict which device will have the more nearly constant resistance as voltage changes.

2. Set Up

Equipment: adjustable low-voltage DC source (or several battery settings), fixed resistor, miniature incandescent bulb, switch, wires, two multimeters or voltage/current sensors.
1
For each device, measure voltage across the device and current through it. Begin at the lowest source setting.
2
