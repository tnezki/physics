Force Model Poster + Peer Presentation

Show that you can build a force model, use net force to explain motion, and repair incorrect physics reasoning.

Name: __________________________Presentation partner: __________________________Date: __________
Your three I-can statements:
1. I can draw a force diagram for an object in a real-world situation.
2. I can decide whether the forces are balanced or unbalanced.
3. I can explain how the net force affects the object's motion and critique an incorrect force explanation and revise it using physics vocabulary.
Your product: Create one 8.5 × 11 page poster that teaches all three I-can statements through one real-world situation. Your poster should be understandable to a student who missed this section. You have 30 minutes to prepare. Then you will join one partner and your pair will present to two classmates.

What must be on your one-page poster?

1 · Real-world situation + force diagram
Choose one object in a clear situation. Draw only the forces acting on that object. Label each force and make arrow directions and relative sizes physically reasonable.
2 · Balanced or unbalanced?
State whether the forces are balanced or unbalanced. Show or describe the net force, including its direction when it is not zero.
3 · What happens to the motion?
Explain what the net force means for the object's motion. Be precise: velocity can stay constant when net force is zero; a nonzero net force causes acceleration.
4 · Critique + revise
Write one believable but incorrect force explanation. Identify the error, then rewrite it correctly using physics vocabulary. Do not make the mistake so obvious that there is nothing to critique.
Physics vocabulary: Use force diagram, balanced forces, unbalanced forces, net force, and acceleration correctly. Add situation-specific terms such as weight, support force, friction, tension, or applied force when they belong in your model.

30-minute prep

0-5 minChoose the situation and object. Rough-sketch the model.
5-18 minBuild the force diagram and balanced/unbalanced explanation.
18-25 minAdd motion reasoning plus the incorrect explanation and revision.
25-30 minCheck physics, clean up the poster, and rehearse what you will say.

Pair presentation · about 3 minutes

After prep, join one partner. Find another pair so your pair has two listeners. Present your two posters as one short explanation.

0:00-1:15 · Presenter A: model, net force, and motion.
1:15-2:30 · Presenter B: model, net force, and motion.
2:30-3:00 · Together: highlight one incorrect explanation and how physics fixes it.

Ready-to-present check

  • One page; readable from a few feet away.
  • One chosen object is clear; every force arrow is a real interaction.
  • Balanced/unbalanced, net force, and motion all agree.
  • Incorrect explanation is specifically critiqued and revised.
  • Both partners speak during the 3-minute presentation.
