Physics - Unit 8 - Section 8.5 - Investigation

Follow the Energy Through the System

Measure transformer behavior, reverse a motor/generator, and audit modern electromagnetic-device claims.

Learning target: Apply fields, forces, current, induction, transformer relationships, power, and energy conservation to electromagnetic systems.
Research Question: How can different electromagnetic devices change voltage, produce motion, or produce electrical output without creating energy?

1. Transformer Prediction

A transformer has 100 primary turns and 300 secondary turns. Predict whether it is step-up or step-down and how its secondary voltage should compare with the primary.

2. Measure a Low-Voltage Transformer

Equipment: low-voltage AC source, iron transformer core, primary and secondary coils with known turn counts, AC voltmeter/multimeter, small resistor/load if directed by your teacher, small DC motor and LED for the later station.

3. Power Does Not Appear for Free

For an idealized transformer, use Pin approximately equals Pout and P = IV. If 60 W enters the transformer and the secondary voltage is 20 V, estimate output power and secondary current.

6. Critique a Magnet-Powered Claim

A device advertisement claims that permanent magnets keep a wheel spinning forever while a generator supplies continuous electrical power with no energy input. Identify the conservation-of-energy problem and name at least two measurements you would require before accepting the claim.
