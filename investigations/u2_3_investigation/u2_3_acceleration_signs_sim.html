What Did You Notice?

Use your recorded signed values to support short explanations.

Six follow-up questions

1. Compare seconds 0 and 10 in Run A. Which direction is the car moving? How do the velocity values show its speed is changing?
2. In Run B, at what second does velocity become 0 m/s? Describe the motion just before and just after that second.
3. In Run C, the velocity is negative while acceleration is positive at the start. Is the car moving left or right? Speeding up or slowing down?
4. Run D has negative velocity and negative acceleration. Explain why the car speeds up even though both values are negative.
5. Compare Runs B and C. Both cars turn around. Which direction does each car move after turning? Use the velocity signs as evidence.
6. A car has v = +3 m/s and a = −1 m/s². Describe its motion now. If acceleration stays the same, what will eventually happen?
