Physics • Unit 2.3 • Investigation Teacher Guide

Which Way? Which Speed?

20 minutes • Walter Fendt Motion with Constant Acceleration • Individual or pairs

Learning focus: The sign of velocity gives the direction of travel. The sign of acceleration gives the direction of velocity change. Same signs → speeding up; opposite signs → slowing down until a turn, then speeding up in the new direction.

Before students begin

  • Open https://www.walter-fendt.de/html5/phen/acceleration_en.htm. Test Reset, Start, Enter after each number, Slow motion, and the checkboxes for the velocity and acceleration vectors.
  • Show the two vertically aligned signed number lines. Students mark v on the top line and a on the bottom line, then circle a prediction and their observation.
  • Emphasize: positive is right, negative is left for both quantities; the arrows mean different things. This is not a calculation lab.

20-minute pacing

TimeTask
0–2 minOpen site, set initial position 0 m, turn on both vectors and slow motion.
2–9 minPage 1: Run A and then Run B. Predict, watch, circle.
9–16 minPage 2: Run C and then Run D. Predict, watch, circle.
16–20 minAnswer two pattern questions; discuss one turning case.

Expected observations

RunStart vaWhat students should see
A+2 m/s+1 m/s²Moving right, speeding up; no turn.
B+2 m/s−1 m/s²Moving right, slowing down; stops momentarily and turns; then moves left, speeding up.
C−2 m/s−1 m/s²Moving left, speeding up; no turn.
D−2 m/s+1 m/s²Moving left, slowing down; stops momentarily and turns; then moves right, speeding up.

Teacher prompts and debrief

  1. Run B: “Before it turns, is it still moving right even though a is negative?” Yes. Opposite signs mean slower, not instantly moving left.
  2. At the turn: velocity is zero for an instant. Acceleration stays negative in Run B; it does not need to flip to turn the car.
  3. After the turn: velocity is now negative, so v and a have the same sign and the car speeds up to the left.
  4. Optionally point to the simulation's velocity–time graph: crossing v = 0 marks a turn; the horizontal acceleration–time graph keeps the same sign.

Expected written responses

1. The sign of velocity tells direction of motion. Example: Run B is still moving right while v is positive, even though acceleration is negative.

2. Run B first moves right and slows. It reaches zero velocity, reverses, and speeds up left. Velocity is negative after the turn while acceleration remains negative.

Troubleshooting: If a car leaves the visible road too quickly, use Slow motion and Reset, then repeat. Input fields require Enter to accept a new number. The movable green/red barriers control their clocks; students do not need clock data for this activity.
