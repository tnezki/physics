Learning target: Explain acceleration as the rate at which velocity changes and identify acceleration from motion descriptions, data, and graphs.

Research question: What evidence shows that a cart on a gentle ramp is accelerating rather than moving at constant velocity?

Prediction:

1. Set Up and Record (Trial 1 • Guided)

Materials: four-foot board, toy car/cart, support blocks, tape, meterstick, iPad camera, Vernier Video Analysis, soft stop.

  1. Mark 0, 1, 2, 3, 4 ft on the ramp. Raise one end a little and secure it. Place a soft stop after 4 ft.
  2. Record the ramp height (vertical height of the raised end). Release the car at 0 ft without pushing.
  3. Film the entire run side-on with the whole ramp and a known measurement visible. Record one good trial.
Trial 1 ramp height: cm

2. Track the Car With Your Teacher

  1. Open the video in Vernier Video Analysis. Set the Scale from a known length on the ramp; set the origin at 0 ft and positive direction down the ramp.
  2. Open Advanced Video Options (gear) → Advance Frames → 5 frames.
  3. Use Add to mark the same point on the car in each selected frame. Continue through the run.
  4. View velocity vs. time. Use the Vernier data table to copy your time, position, and velocity readings below.
