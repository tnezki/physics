Physics - Unit 3 - Section 3.1 - Investigation

Force, Mass, and Acceleration: Test the Second Law

Use measured force and motion data to test the two patterns inside Newton's Second Law.

Learning target: Students will explain how net force and mass determine acceleration and use Newton's Second Law to analyze changes in motion.
Research Question: How do changes in net force and mass change a cart's acceleration?

1. Predict the Two Patterns

Without calculating, predict what happens to acceleration when (A) the net force is increased while mass stays the same and (B) mass is increased while the net force stays about the same.

2. Set Up the Cart and Sensors

Equipment: low-friction cart and level track, added masses, Vernier motion detector, Vernier force sensor, string, Graphical Analysis.
1
Place the motion detector at one end of the track with a clear view of the cart. Keep the cart at least about 0.5 m from the detector.
2
Attach the force sensor to the cart with string so the cart can be pulled horizontally. Zero the force sensor while the string is slack.
3
Display force vs. time and velocity vs. time. Use a linear fit on the nearly straight part of the velocity graph to estimate acceleration.

On this low-friction track, treat the measured horizontal pull as a close approximation to the horizontal net force. Section 3.2 will add friction as an important real-world correction.
