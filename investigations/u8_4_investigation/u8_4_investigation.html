Physics - Unit 8 - Section 8.4 - Investigation

Move, Hold, Reverse: Induction

Use a coil and meter to determine exactly when changing magnetic conditions produce voltage.

Learning target: Explain electromagnetic induction, the rate-of-change relationship, generator energy conversion, and alternating voltage.
Research Question: Which changes in magnet-coil motion produce induced voltage, and what controls its direction and size?

1. Predict the Meter Response

A bar magnet will move into, stop inside, and move out of a coil connected to a center-zero galvanometer or sensitive voltmeter. Predict when the meter will move and which actions should reverse the deflection.

Equipment: bar magnet, multi-turn coil, center-zero galvanometer or sensitive voltmeter, connecting leads, stopwatch.

4. State the Faraday Relationship

Use words, not a new algebraic equation: how does induced voltage depend on the rate of magnetic-field change?

5. Build an AC Model

Imagine the magnet moves into and out of the coil repeatedly at a steady rate. Sketch a qualitative voltage-time graph showing repeated positive and negative response. Label one cycle and two zero crossings.

6. Energy Audit

Explain why a generator does not create energy. Identify the mechanical input, the changing magnetic condition, the induced electrical output, and what must happen if the mechanical motion stops.
