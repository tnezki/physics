Learning Target: Represent forces as vectors and determine resultant/net force in simple force situations.

For every scenario, work before checking.

  1. Draw the given force vectors from one origin on the grid.
  2. Construct and predict the resultant vector R.
  3. Show the math or geometric reasoning that supports your prediction.
  4. Recreate the vectors in PhET Vector Addition and record the checked resultant.
Angles are measured counterclockwise from +x.
0° = right · 90° = up
180° = left · 270° = down

Choose a drawing scale that fits the grid.
