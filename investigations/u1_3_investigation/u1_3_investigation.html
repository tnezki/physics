Build the Resultant

Use the PhET Vector Addition simulation to predict, build, and test net-force vectors.

Learning target: Represent forces as vectors and determine the direction and relative size of the net force in one-dimensional and simple two-dimensional situations.
Research Question: How do the direction and length of individual force vectors determine the resultant (net-force) vector?

Simulation: PhET Vector Addition. Use Explore 1D and Explore 2D. Show values when the directions ask you to verify a prediction.
