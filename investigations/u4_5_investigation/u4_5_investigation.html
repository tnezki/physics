Physics - Unit 4 - Section 5 - Investigation

Energy Decision Board

Use multiple criteria to evaluate energy options and critique an impossible claim.

Learning target: Evaluate energy sources, carriers, transformations, efficiency, and real-world energy decisions using conservation of energy.
Research Question: How can evidence and conservation of energy support a defensible energy decision?

1. Community Scenario

A fictional community must choose a primary electricity strategy for a new public facility. No option is perfect. Your team must use evidence, state tradeoffs, and defend a recommendation.

Decision criteria: local availability, reliability, environmental impact, efficiency / useful output, and infrastructure needs.

5. Claim Check: “Free Energy”

A vendor says a device can run forever after one initial push and continuously supply extra useful energy with no further input. List the energy measurements or evidence you would require before accepting the claim, then explain the conservation problem.

A defensible recommendation identifies tradeoffs. The goal is not to produce one universal “best” energy source.
