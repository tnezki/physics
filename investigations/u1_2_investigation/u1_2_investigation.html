Part 6 · Synthesize

CER Claim: "Inertia should be drawn as a force acting on an object."

Support or reject the claim.

Claim:

Evidence: Use specific evidence from at least two sources today: the coin/card test, the PhET cases, or the book.

Reasoning: Connect your evidence to Newton's First Law, net force, and what inertia means.

Closing Discussion

  1. If inertia is not a force, what does it describe?
  2. What evidence from today distinguishes no forces from no net force?
  3. Why should every force arrow be connected to a real interaction?
  4. Why might the slow pull and fast flick give different coin motion?
  5. What must be true about the net force for an object's motion to change?
