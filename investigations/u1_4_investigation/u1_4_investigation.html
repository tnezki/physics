Synthesis

What does equilibrium actually mean?

1

Make a claim from evidence

A student says, “If an object is at rest, either no forces act on it or every support force must be the same size.” Use evidence from this investigation to respond.

2

Write the equilibrium rule

Use words and an equation to state the rule for vertical equilibrium.

3

Final prediction

A suspended system has a mass of \(0.650\text{ kg}\). Support A reads \(2.40\text{ N}\). If the system is in vertical equilibrium, predict the reading on Support B. Show enough work to make your reasoning clear.

4

Evaluate the model

Your calculated and measured forces may not match perfectly. Identify one reasonable source of experimental difference without changing the equilibrium model.

Connection: Today your system was at rest, so you observed static equilibrium. An object moving in a straight line at constant velocity can also have zero net force; that is dynamic equilibrium. In both cases, equilibrium means the velocity is not changing.
