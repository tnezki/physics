Physics - Unit 3 - Section 3.4 - Investigation

Equal Forces, Different Motion

Measure paired forces, then use unequal-mass carts to separate force equality from acceleration equality.

Learning target: Students will identify force interactions and explain Newton's Third Law using action-reaction pairs that act on different objects.
Research Question: How can interaction forces be equal and opposite while the two objects respond differently?

1. Predict the Force Pair

Two spring scales are hooked together. Student A pulls left while Student B pulls right. If Scale A reads 4 N, what should Scale B read? Predict first.
