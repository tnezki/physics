Physics - Unit 2 - Section 5 - Investigation Teacher Guide

Vernier motion-model synthesis investigation

Vernier motion-model synthesis investigation - designed for 30 minutes

Time30 minutes
Groups3-4
Core toolMotion detector + Graphical Analysis

Why This Investigation Fits Section 2.5

Use velocity vectors, motion diagrams, and simple graphs to model and explain the motion of objects. The activity comes after the notes, so students use the section model to interpret evidence rather than discovering later-unit content prematurely.

Materials Per Group

  • Vernier motion detector, Graphical Analysis, meter stick or floor markers, open walking lane

Before Class

  1. Test the motion detector and prepare 3-5 story cards or assign stories verbally.

Suggested Flow

MinutesStudent WorkTeacher Focus
0-5Prediction and setupCheck that the research question and variable choices are understood.
5-18Collect evidencePrioritize clean, interpretable evidence over many trials.
18-25Analyze / comparePress students to cite data, graph features, or observations.
25-30Conclude / modelRequire a conclusion that directly answers the question.

Expected Evidence

Students should coordinate dot spacing, graph trends, vector length/direction, and written motion. A change in vector length or direction should align with evidence of changing velocity in the graph/story.

Watch for: Students may make a graph that looks close while their motion diagram or velocity arrows tell a different story; require cross-checking.

Strong Responses

FeatureWhat to Look For
Claim / conclusionDirectly answers the research question rather than restating procedure.
EvidenceUses specific measured values, graph trends, or observed relationships.
ReasoningUses only the Section 2.5 physics model and connects evidence to the claim.

Common Problems

  • Messy data: Repeat one clean trial instead of averaging several poor runs.
  • Direction confusion: Have students state the chosen positive direction or reference frame before interpreting signs or motion.
  • Overreach: If students introduce later concepts, redirect them to the representations and formulas currently established in Unit 2.

Safety / Cleanup

Keep walking and drop zones clear, use non-breakable equipment, and reset sensors/carts/materials for the next class. For the free-fall investigation, do not allow students to climb on desks or chairs.

Connection Forward

This investigation closes Unit 2 by requiring multiple representations to agree with one evidence-based motion story.
