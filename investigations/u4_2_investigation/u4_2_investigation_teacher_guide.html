Physics - Unit 4 - Section 2 - Investigation Teacher Guide

Momentum Before and After

Video-analysis cart collision - designed for 25-30 minutes

Time25-30 minutes
Groups2-3 students
Core toolGraphical Analysis video analysis

Why This Investigation Fits Section 4.2

Section 4.2 requires students to choose a system and compare total momentum before and after collisions. A sticking collision is a clean test because the after-state has one shared velocity while kinetic energy need not be conserved.

Materials Per Group

  • Two dynamics carts with sticking bumpers
  • Level track
  • Balance
  • Meter stick for scale
  • iPad/phone with Vernier Graphical Analysis video analysis

Before Class

  1. Level the track and measure/label each cart mass if time is tight.
  2. Set a fixed side-view camera position; avoid panning.
  3. Verify that the carts stick reliably at a moderate speed.

Suggested Flow

MinutesStudent WorkTeacher Focus
0-4Prediction + system choiceDefine positive direction and choose both carts as the system.
4-10Record collisionGet one clean clip with scale visible.
10-20Video velocities + p calculationsStudents calculate each contribution with p=mv and total before/after.
20-28Evaluate conservationDiscuss mismatch as external impulse/measurement uncertainty, not as a reason to force equality.

Expected Evidence

Total momentum before and after should be reasonably close, while the pair moves more slowly after sticking. Data will not be exact because of friction, video tracking, and collision timing.

Watch for: Momentum uses velocity with direction. Students who drop signs can make an opposite-direction trial appear to violate conservation.

Common Problems

  • Perspective error: keep camera perpendicular and scale in the motion plane.
  • Tracking through impact: use frames clearly before and clearly after; the collision frames are noisy.
  • Carts drift: re-level the track.

Strong Responses

PromptWhat to Look For
System reasoningBoth carts included; cart-cart forces identified as internal; friction/table effects as external.
Conservation claimUses measured total p values and acknowledges experimental mismatch without claiming exact identity.

Connection Forward
