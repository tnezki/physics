Physics - Unit 7 - Section 7.2 - Investigation

What Flows? What Changes?

Measure a simple circuit to separate charge flow from energy-per-charge change.

Learning target: Explain voltage, current, complete circuits, and the difference between charge flow and energy transfer.
Research Question: Does a load use up current, or does a different electrical quantity change across the device?

1. Predict Before Measuring

In a one-bulb circuit, predict whether the current measured just before the bulb will be larger than, smaller than, or about the same as the current just after the bulb. Then predict how the battery voltage compares with the voltage across the bulb while it is operating.

2. Build a Complete Circuit

Equipment: low-voltage DC battery pack, miniature bulb or approved resistor load, switch, connecting wires, digital multimeter(s).
1
Build battery - switch - load - battery in one closed loop. Keep the switch open until the teacher checks the circuit.
2
Close the switch and confirm the load operates. Open the switch before moving meter leads.

5. Use the Section Relationships

If your measured current is I, calculate the amount of charge that passes one point in 10.0 s using I = Q/t. Then interpret your measured source voltage as joules of energy per coulomb using V = E/q.

6. Defend a Model

Use your evidence to correct this claim: “The battery sends current to the bulb, and the bulb uses the current up.” Distinguish what flows from what energy is transferred.
