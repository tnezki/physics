Physics - Unit 8 - Section 8.2 - Investigation

Wire, Loop, Coil: Shape the Field

Use compass evidence to connect current direction and conductor geometry to magnetic-field patterns.

Learning target: Explain that moving electric charge produces magnetic fields and compare the fields around a straight wire, loop, and coil.
Research Question: How do current direction and wire shape change the magnetic field that a compass detects?

1. Predict Three Field Shapes

Predict how compass evidence will differ around (A) a straight current-carrying wire, (B) one loop, and (C) a many-turn coil.
